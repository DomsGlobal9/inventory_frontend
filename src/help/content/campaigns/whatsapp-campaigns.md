A campaign is one message sent to many customers. Each customer gets it **from your shop's own WhatsApp number**, with their own name in it, as if you had sent it by hand. It can carry a picture of what is on offer, and a link.

Before your first campaign:

1. Link your shop's WhatsApp in **Settings** → **WhatsApp**.
2. Make sure customers have agreed to hear from you (step 1 below).

## 1. Only customers who agreed

A campaign reaches only customers who said yes to offers on WhatsApp. Ask them first, then record it:

- at the counter, in **New sale**: tick **Agrees to offers on WhatsApp**;
- on the customer's page: tick [[1]] **Agrees to offers on WhatsApp** in the **Offers on WhatsApp** card.

![The Offers on WhatsApp card on a customer's page, with Agrees to offers on WhatsApp ticked and marked 1.](1-agrees.webp "Tick it only when the customer said yes.")

Who recorded the yes, and when, is kept. The law on personal data (the DPDP Act) asks you to be able to show that a customer agreed.

Every message ends with *Reply STOP to stop these messages.* You cannot remove this line. A customer who replies STOP never gets another message from your number through ScaleEzy, not even a bill, and their page says so. This cannot be switched back on.

## 2. Write a campaign

1. In the menu on the left, click **Campaigns**, then **New campaign**.
2. Choose [[1]] **Start blank**, or [[2]] one of the templates: the four starters, or your own saved ones.

![Starting a new campaign. Start blank is marked 1 and the Festival offer starter 2.](5-templates.webp "Your own templates show above the starters.")

3. Give it a name that only you see, for example *Dasara silk week*.
4. [[1]] Write the message. [[2]] The buttons under it add words that are filled in for each customer:

| Button | Becomes |
|---|---|
| First name | The customer's first name: *Lakshmi* |
| Shop name | Your shop's name |
| Their points | Their loyalty points |
| Points worth ₹ | What those points are worth |
| Link | The customer's own short link (only when you choose a link, step 3) |

5. [[3]] Choose who gets it: **Everyone who agreed**, **Bought recently** or **Haven't bought for a while**. You can narrow it further by customer group (for example VIP), by how much they have spent, or by points.
6. [[4]] The count under the choices shows how many it reaches right now, with a few names. Under it, the page says who is left out and why: customers who have not agreed to offers, who replied STOP, or who have no phone number.
7. On the right is exactly what the customer will see. Click **Send me a test** to get it on your own shop's WhatsApp first.
8. Click **Save draft**.

![New campaign. The message is marked 1, the First name button 2, Everyone who agreed 3 and Reaches 4 customers 4. On the right is the message as a customer sees it.](2-write.webp "The STOP line is always added at the end.")

## 3. A picture and a link

Both are optional, and both are in the same **New campaign** box.

The picture goes above the words, as a photo does in WhatsApp.

1. Click [[1]] **Upload a photo**, or **Choose a product photo** and pick one of your products' photos.
2. ScaleEzy makes a copy that suits WhatsApp: smaller, turned the right way up, and without the hidden details a phone photo carries (such as where it was taken). What you see is what customers get.

The link lets a customer answer the offer with one tap:

1. Choose [[2]] **Chat with the shop**: the link opens a WhatsApp chat with your shop, with a first line already typed for them (*Hi, I saw your offer…*). Check the number: it is your shop's phone from Settings.
2. Or choose **A web page** and type its address, for example the product on your own website. It must start with `https://`.
3. Put the link where it should go in the words with [[3]] **Link**. Choosing a link adds it at the end for you.

![New campaign with a picture and a chat link. The picture is marked 1, Chat with the shop 2 and the Link button 3. On the right, the picture is above the words and the link is in them.](6-picture-link.webp "Each customer gets their own link, so you can see who tapped it.")

Each customer gets **their own** short link, like `go.scaleezy.com/x7Kq9Pm`. That is how the campaign page knows who tapped it. A link works for 90 days (you can choose 30 to 365), and then shows *This offer has ended*.

Under a picture WhatsApp allows 1,024 letters. The counter under the message counts against it once a picture is chosen, allowing for long names, the link and the STOP line. Name what is on offer in the words too (*15% off Kanchipuram silk sarees*), in case the picture does not load on a customer's phone.

:::note
**Send me a test** sends the real picture and a real link. The test's link is marked as a test, so taps on it are not counted in the campaign.
:::

## 4. Start it

Open the draft and check the message and the count, then click **Start**, and [[1]] **Start sending** in the box that asks.

![The box that asks before a campaign starts, saying how many customers, how fast, and that the words cannot change after. Start sending is marked 1.](3-start.webp "You can pause or stop it at any time.")

Once started, the message, the picture, the link and the list of customers are fixed. The campaign page shows *What was sent*, even if you later change a product or your shop's name. To change the words, click **Copy** to make a new draft.

With a link, the campaign first shows **Getting links ready** while each customer's link is made. Nobody is sent a message without their link.

To send later, tick **Send later** when writing it and choose the day and time. It still goes only between 10 am and 8 pm.

:::warning
Campaigns go out **slowly, on purpose**. A normal WhatsApp number that suddenly sends hundreds of messages is what WhatsApp blocks, and then your bills and orders would stop too. So:

- at most **20 a day** for a number linked in the last two weeks, then at most **100 a day**;
- only between **10 am and 8 pm**;
- bills and purchase orders always go first;
- a customer gets **at most one offer from you in any 3 days**. If their turn in a campaign comes within 3 days of another offer, they are skipped. Birthday and anniversary wishes and points reminders do not count.

A campaign to 300 customers therefore takes a few days. The page says how long before you start.
:::

## 5. Follow it

The campaign page shows [[2]] how many were sent, delivered and read, and [[3]] each customer's own line: **Waiting its turn**, **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Read**, **Not sent** or **Skipped**, with the reason. Under the numbers it counts why some were not sent, for example *2 not sent: Had an offer from you in the last 3 days*.

- [[1]] **Pause** stops sending for now; **Carry on** starts again.
- **Stop** ends it for good. Messages already with WhatsApp still arrive; nobody else gets it.

![A campaign that is sending. Pause is marked 1, How it is going 2, and a customer's Waiting its turn 3.](4-follow-it.webp "Each customer's line changes as WhatsApp sends, delivers and reads it.")

With a link, the page also shows [[1]] how many customers **tapped the link**, and each one who did is marked **Tapped**. A customer who taps three times counts once; the total taps are shown beside it. WhatsApp itself opens every link to draw its preview, and those opens are not counted.

If the link was wrong (a wrong price, a wrong page), click [[2]] **Switch links off**. Customers who tap then see *This promotion is no longer available*. **Switch links on** turns them back on.

![A campaign with a picture and a link. 0 customers tapped the link is marked 1 and Switch links off 2.](7-tapped.webp "Switching the links off does not stop the campaign; Stop does.")

## 6. Templates

To use a campaign again, click **Save as template** on its page, or at the bottom of the **New campaign** box. A template keeps the words, the picture and the link, not who it goes to. Your templates show when you click **New campaign**, above the starters. To delete one, click the bin beside it.

## 7. Automatic messages

In **Settings** → **Loyalty & wishes** you can switch on messages that go by themselves, from 10 am on the day:

- **Birthday wishes** and **Anniversary wishes**, for customers with the date saved on their page (**Change dates** on the **Offers on WhatsApp** card). Each can have a picture above it: **Picture above the wish**;
- a reminder a week before a customer's loyalty points lapse;
- a thank-you after each sale that says how many points they earned and now have.

Each day's wishes appear under **Campaigns** → **Automatic messages**, with the same ticks as a campaign. See [Loyalty points](/help/customers/loyalty-points) for points.

:::faq The count says 0 customers
Nobody who agreed to offers matches your choices. Check the choices, and tick **Agrees to offers on WhatsApp** for customers who said yes.
:::

:::faq It has been started but nothing is sent
Check the first box on the Campaigns page. If your shop's WhatsApp is not linked, the campaign waits until it is. Outside 10 am to 8 pm, or once today's number of messages has gone, it waits for the next day.
:::

:::faq Why was a customer skipped with "Had an offer from you in the last 3 days"?
They were sent another campaign less than 3 days before their turn came. A customer gets at most one offer from you in any 3 days, so your number is not reported as spam.
:::

:::faq My photo was refused
Use a JPEG or PNG under 15 MB. An iPhone HEIC photo must be shared as a JPEG first (or set the camera to **Most Compatible**). Very small pictures (under 100 pixels) are refused too.
:::

:::faq Can I send a PDF in a campaign?
No. A campaign can carry one picture. Bills and other documents are sent from their own pages.
:::
