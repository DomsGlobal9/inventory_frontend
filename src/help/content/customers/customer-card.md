When a customer walks in, ask "Have you shopped with us before?" and type their number. The card shows who they are and what to show them first.

## Open it

1. Go to **Customers** and press **Customer card**.
2. Type the customer's mobile number and press **Look up**.

On a phone, keep the page open: one number after another, no need to go back.

![The Customer card for a regular customer: her VIP badge, last visit, sizes, colours, usual spend, and items in her size in this store.](1-card.webp "Everything comes from her own bills. Nothing is guessed.")

## What the card shows

Everything they bought counts: at the till, in your online shop and on Shopify. An online order where they typed this number counts too, even if they did not confirm it with the code.

- **Name and badges**: their groups, like VIP, the last visit, how many bills, and their points. A customer who has only [bills from before ScaleEzy](/help/customers/customers#bills-from-before-scaleezy) says **Past customer**, with how many earlier bills: those bills have no dates.
- **Size**: the size they buy for each kind of garment, for example M for kurtis and Free for sarees.
- **Colours** and **Buys**: what they come back for most.
- **Usually spends**: what most of their bills come to. Show things in that range first.
- **In this store today, in their size**: up to six items you have here now, in their size, their colours first. Items they already bought are left out. The shelf is shown when the store uses [shelves](/help/shelves/where-is-it).
- **Looked at and tried on lately**: pieces they opened, tried on or hearted in your [online shop](/help/online-shop/what-customers-see) or at the try-on screen in the last 30 days, hearted ones first, with how many you have here and the shelf. Only after they confirmed their number there with the WhatsApp code. Anything they bought since is left out. This shows even for a number that is not a customer yet, so you see what a first-time visitor tried before you add them.
- **Others who bought what they bought, also bought**: pieces at least 2 other customers bought together with something they bought. Only items in stock here, in their size. These two show only when there is something to show.

The card reads the store chosen at the top of the screen. Change the store to see what fits them there.

How the card reads their bills: anything they returned does not count, recent buying counts for more than old (a purchase counts half as much for every year since), and colours are counted by family, so Navy, Indigo and Peacock all count as Blue. A colour counts both by how often they buy it and by how much they spend on it, so one costly red lehenga is not outvoted by two cheap dupattas. Items within what they usually spend come first; much pricier or much cheaper ones come after.

## Customers who scan at the door

Press **Door QR** on the Customer card page, print it and stand it at the door. A customer scans it with their phone camera, WhatsApp opens with a message ready, and they press send. They get a short welcome from your shop's number, and they show under **In the store now** on every salesperson's Customer card for two hours.

### In the store now

Customers who scanned are listed with how long they have been **Waiting**, the longest first.

- **I'll take them** opens their card. Everyone else sees *With you* or *With Kiran*, so two people never walk up to the same customer.
- Past **5 minutes** with nobody taking them, the row turns red, the owner gets a bell, and the customer gets one short "sorry for the wait" message.
- Above the list, each salesperson shows as *free* or *with 2*, so nobody walks across the floor to ask.
- **Asked for you**: the customer asked for you, at the door or because you helped them last time. If someone else presses **I'll take them**, they are asked first: **Take them anyway**, or leave them for that person.
- **They asked for…** records who the customer asked for, or **Nobody in particular**.
- **Pass to…** hands the customer to someone else. They see *Passed to you*.
- **Done** when you have finished with them. A bill for them does it too.

**Pass to…** and **Done** are for the person with the customer, and for the owner.

The QR works once your shop's WhatsApp number is linked in **Settings → WhatsApp**. Each store has its own QR: print one for each store's door.

## Notes

Write what helps next time, like "buys for her daughter" or "prefers cotton in summer", and press **Add note**. Each note keeps its date and who wrote it. The same notes show on the customer's own page.

## Shops on ScaleEzy CRM

The card stays here for every shop. If your shop uses ScaleEzy CRM, it also has **Full profile in CRM**, for follow-ups and the full history. **Open their full page** opens their page in Inventory.

## A new customer

If nobody has the number yet, the card says **New customer**. Ask what they are looking for. Press **Add customer**: the number is already filled in. From their first bill, they have a card too.

## Common problems

:::faq The card says "Not known yet"
The customer has no bills yet, so there are no sizes or colours to show. They fill in from the first bill.
:::

:::faq "Nothing here in their size"
Nothing in this store is in their size in a garment they buy, or they already have it. Ask what they need today, or change the store at the top.
:::

:::faq I cannot add a note
Adding notes needs permission to change customers. The owner, an admin and sales staff have it.
:::
