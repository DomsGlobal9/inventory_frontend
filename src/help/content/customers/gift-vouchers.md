A gift voucher is money a customer pays now and someone spends later. One person buys it, often for someone else, and it can be used over several bills until nothing is left.

## Switch it on

1. Go to **Settings → Gift vouchers**.
2. Tick **Sell gift vouchers at the till**.
3. Choose how many months a voucher is valid, and the smallest and largest amount you sell.
4. Press **Save**.

Vouchers already sold can always be used, even if you switch selling off later.

## Selling and using one

Both happen at the POS till.

- **Selling**: the customer pays by cash, UPI or card, and the till prints a code like **GV-7KQM-3XPA**. Selling a voucher is not a sale of goods, so there is no GST on it. GST is charged on the goods when the voucher is used.
- **Using**: the salesperson types or scans the code at payment. Any amount up to what is left can be used, and two vouchers can pay for one bill.
- **Cash limit**: ₹2,00,000 or more in cash for one voucher is always refused. That is the law (section 269ST), not a shop setting.

Every till in every store sees the same balance, so a voucher bought in one store can be used in another.

## Returns

If a bill was paid with a voucher and goods come back, that part of the money goes back onto the same voucher, not into the customer's hand. If the voucher has already expired, it gets 30 more days so the customer can still use it.

## See every voucher

Go to **Customers** and press **Gift vouchers**.

- At the top: what your shop **still owes** on open vouchers, what was **sold this month**, and how many **expire in the next 30 days**.
- Search by code, phone number or name. Choose **Open**, **Used up**, **Expired** or **Blocked** to narrow the list.
- Press a voucher to see its history: when it was sold and how it was paid, every bill it was used on, and anything returned onto it.

## Extend or block a voucher

People with permission to manage gift vouchers can:

- **Extend**: give an open voucher 1 to 12 more months. This must be done before it expires. Once expired, the unused money stays with the shop.
- **Block**: stop a lost or stolen voucher from being used. Write why. **Unblock** brings it back with the same balance.

Every change is written in the voucher's history with who did it.

## In the Day Book

The Day Book has a **Gift vouchers** section: sold that day and how it was paid, used on bills, returned onto vouchers, and what is still owed. Cash paid for vouchers is counted in the cash that should be in the drawer.

## Who can do what

- **See gift vouchers** lets someone open the list and the history.
- **Manage gift vouchers** also lets them change the settings, extend and block.

Owners and admins have both. Sales staff can see them. Change this in **Settings → Roles**.
