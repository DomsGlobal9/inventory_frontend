Loyalty points are a thank-you to customers who come back. They earn points on what they pay, and later use those points to pay part of a bill.

Points work only at the counter, in [New sale](/help/orders/new-sale).

## 1. Switch points on

1. In the menu on the left, click **Settings**, then **Loyalty & wishes**.
2. Tick **Loyalty points**.
3. Choose the rules, then click **Save**.

| Rule | What it means | Usual choice |
|---|---|---|
| Points for every ₹100 | Points earned for each full ₹100 paid | 1 |
| One point is worth | What one point takes off a bill | ₹1 |
| Can use points from | Points a customer must hold before they can use any | 100 |
| Points can pay up to | The most of one bill that points can pay | 50% |
| Points lapse after | Months with no purchase before the points lapse (0 = never) | 12 |

The line under the rules shows what they give, for example *A ₹5,000 bill earns 50 points, worth ₹50 on a later bill — 1% back.*

:::note
Only owners, and roles with the loyalty permission, can change these rules. Everyone who takes sales can give and take points at the till.
:::

## 2. At the counter

Type the customer's phone number in **New sale** as usual. For a customer who already has points, the payment box shows how many they have and what they are worth, and how many this bill earns.

- To use points, type them in **Points to use**, or click the button that uses the most allowed on this bill.
- The rest of the bill is paid as usual: cash, UPI, card or split.
- The **Complete sale** button shows both, for example *₹2,250 + 250 points*.

The receipt shows the points as a payment, **Loyalty points**, next to the cash or UPI.

Points are earned only on what was paid in money. A bill of ₹2,500 with 250 points used earns points on ₹2,250.

## 3. A customer's points

Open the customer from **Customers**. The **Loyalty points** card shows:

- how many points they have and what they are worth;
- the day they lapse if the customer does not come back;
- every change: earned on a bill, used on a bill, given or taken back by a return, a birthday gift, lapsed, or changed by hand.

To add or take away points by hand (a goodwill gesture, or a mistake), click **Change points**, type how many and why, and save. The reason is kept with the change. Points can never go below zero this way.

## 4. Returns

When a return is completed, points follow the goods:

- Points used on the returned items go back to the customer **as points**, not as money.
- Points earned on the returned items are taken back.

While the return is still open, its page already says how the refund splits, for example *250 points (₹250) go back to the customer's points*, and shows only the money part as **Refund owed to the customer**. Pay back only that amount in money.

## 5. Birthday gift and lapsing points

- **Birthday gift**: in **Loyalty & wishes**, tick **Birthday wishes** and type how many points to give. Customers with a birthday saved on their page get them once a year, on the day.
- **Lapsing**: points lapse when a customer has not bought or used points for the months you chose. Tick **Remind customers before their points lapse** to send a WhatsApp a week before.

See [WhatsApp campaigns](/help/campaigns/whatsapp-campaigns) for the wishes and reminders.

:::faq The till says points can be used from 100
The customer holds fewer points than **Can use points from**. They keep earning; once they reach it, points can be used.
:::

:::faq The till will not let me use all their points
Points can pay only part of a bill: **Points can pay up to** in the rules. The button shows the most allowed on this bill.
:::

:::faq Two counters used the same customer's points at once
Only one sale gets them. The other is told the customer does not have that many points any more, and nothing on that bill is saved.
:::

:::faq Can a balance go below zero?
Only through a return: when points earned on returned goods were already spent, the balance can show a small minus. Their next purchase fills it again.
:::
