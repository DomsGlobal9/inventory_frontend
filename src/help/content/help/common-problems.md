Can't find your problem here? Search the guide with **Ctrl K**, or ask ScaleEzy in [Settings → Help & Support](/help/settings/get-support).

:::note First check these two things
Most problems are one of these:
1. **The wrong store is picked** at the top of the screen. Stock, sales and shelves always belong to that store.
2. **Your role doesn't include it.** If a menu item or button is missing, ask your shop owner. They decide what each role can do in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
:::

## Signing in

:::faq I forgot my password
ScaleEzy doesn't send reset emails. Your shop owner or manager sets a new password for you in **Settings → Team & Users**. [Full answer](/help/start/sign-in#forgot-your-password)
:::

:::faq "Invalid credentials"
The email or password is wrong, or your account has been switched off. Check for a space before or after the email and that Caps Lock is off, then ask your owner. [Full answer](/help/start/sign-in#invalid-credentials)
:::

:::faq I was signed out by myself
Your password was changed, your account was switched off, or someone chose **Sign out other devices**. Sign in again with your current password. [Full answer](/help/start/sign-in#i-was-signed-out-by-myself)
:::

## Menus and buttons

:::faq A menu item or button is missing
The person's role doesn't include it. The owner edits the role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**, presses **Save role**, and the person refreshes ScaleEzy. [Full answer](/help/settings/roles#a-staff-member-says-a-menu-item-is-missing)
:::

:::faq A button is grey and can't be pressed
Something the screen needs is not filled in yet. Read the short message near the button: it usually says what is missing. Each guide page lists its own grey-button cases under *Common problems*.
:::

:::faq My computer beeps and "New Inventory Alert!" pops up
A new low stock or out of stock alert has come in. Click **View Now** to see it, or the **×** to close the message. It beeps only once for each new alert. [Full answer](/help/start/find-your-way#alerts)
:::

:::faq The barcode scanner does nothing
Click in the box first, then scan again: the scanner types where the cursor is. If the code shows but nothing is found, the scanner may not be sending **Enter**. [Full answer](/help/start/barcode-scanner#i-scan-and-nothing-happens)
:::

## Selling

:::faq I don't see New sale
Your role can't sell at the counter, or you are on a phone. New sale works on a computer or tablet. [Full answer](/help/orders/new-sale#i-don-t-see-new-sale)
:::

:::faq Complete sale stays grey
Read the line under the button. It says what is missing, for example the customer's name or phone, or why money is coming off the bill. [Full answer](/help/orders/new-sale#complete-sale-stays-grey)
:::

:::faq "Prices changed. Check the bill, then press Complete sale again."
A price or offer changed while you were billing. Check the new **To pay** and press **Complete sale** again. [Full answer](/help/orders/new-sale#prices-changed-check-the-bill-then-press-complete-sale-again)
:::

:::faq I can't find an order
Clear the search box and set the lists back to **All statuses** and **From anywhere**, then search with fewer digits or only the first name. [Full answer](/help/orders/find-an-order#i-can-t-find-an-order)
:::

:::faq A counter sale was a mistake
A counter sale is complete as soon as it is made, so it can't be cancelled. Book the pieces back in as a return. [Full answer](/help/orders/cancel-an-order#a-counter-sale-was-a-mistake)
:::

## Returns

:::faq I don't see the Return button
Your role can't log returns, or every piece has already come back and the row says **All returned**. [Full answer](/help/returns/book-a-return#i-don-t-see-the-return-button)
:::

:::faq I chose Restock but the piece is damaged
Before **Complete Return**, click **Inspect Items** and change it. After completing, the piece is already back in stock, so take it out with **Remove stock**. [Full answer](/help/returns/finish-a-return#i-chose-restock-but-the-piece-is-damaged)
:::

## Stock

:::faq The stock number is different from what is on the shelf
Check the store at the top first. If it is still wrong, count the pieces and correct the stock. [Full answer](/help/inventory/see-your-stock#the-number-is-different-from-what-is-on-the-shelf)
:::

:::faq The numbers look different in another store
Each store has its own stock, sales and shelves. Check the store name at the top of the screen. A product's **Total Stock** adds up all your stores. [Full answer](/help/start/more-than-one-store#the-numbers-look-wrong)
:::

:::faq "Only 2 of this item are free here: 3 are held for orders"
Some pieces are held for confirmed orders that haven't been sent yet. Send or cancel those orders first, or use fewer pieces. [Full answer](/help/inventory/remove-stock#only-2-of-this-item-are-free-here-3-are-held-for-orders)
:::

:::faq An item is low but has no alert
Check its reorder level: an item with a reorder level of 0 is never marked low, only out of stock. Also check the store at the top. [Full answer](/help/inventory/low-stock-alerts#an-item-is-low-but-has-no-alert)
:::

:::faq Where do I set the reorder level?
Each item has its own reorder level, the number at which it counts as low. It is the same in every store. Change it from a spreadsheet with a **reorderLevel** column in **Import Updates** on Products. [Full answer](/help/inventory/low-stock-alerts#set-the-reorder-level)
:::

:::faq "No stock available at this location to transfer"
The store you chose to move from has no pieces. Check the **Origin Location**. [Full answer](/help/transfers/transfers#no-stock-available-at-this-location-to-transfer)
:::

## Shelves

:::faq The item is not in the Put away list
All of it is already on shelves, or it was received at a different store. Check the store at the top. [Full answer](/help/shelves/put-away#the-item-is-not-in-the-list)
:::

:::faq The shelf says 3 but I only find 2
Someone may have moved pieces without recording it. Look on nearby shelves. If they are really missing, count the shelf so the difference is recorded. [Full answer](/help/shelves/where-is-it#the-shelf-says-3-but-i-only-find-2)
:::

:::faq "This label belongs to another store"
You are working in a different store. Change the store at the top of the screen, then scan again. [Full answer](/help/shelves/put-away#this-label-belongs-to-another-store)
:::

## Products

:::faq PUBLISH PRODUCT is grey
One item on the publishing checklist isn't ticked, most often the photo. Add it, or save as a draft and add the photo later. [Full answer](/help/products/add-a-product#publish-product-is-grey)
:::

:::faq My product is not in the Products list
Check the filter next to **Export**: **Active & Drafts** hides archived and trashed products. The list also shows at most 50 products. [Full answer](/help/products/publish-archive-delete#my-product-is-not-in-the-products-list)
:::

:::faq I can't change a product's name
A product's name can't be changed after it is saved. Selling prices can still be changed on the product's **Variants** tab. [Full answer](/help/products/add-a-product#can-i-change-a-product-s-name-or-price-after-saving)
:::

:::faq "SKU not found" when importing updates
The SKU in your file doesn't match any item. Check the spelling, or copy the exact SKU from the product's **Variants** tab. [Full answer](/help/products/bulk-updates#sku-not-found)
:::

:::faq The counter still shows the old price
A store price only applies in that one store. Check the store at the top, and that you pressed **Save** in the right store's box. [Full answer](/help/products/price-per-store#the-counter-still-shows-the-old-price)
:::

## Purchase orders

:::faq There is no Receive Now column
The order is still a **DRAFT** (it must be sent first) or it is already fully **RECEIVED**. [Full answer](/help/purchase-orders/receive-a-delivery#there-is-no-receive-now-column)
:::

:::faq "Cannot receive more than remaining quantity"
You typed more than is still waiting on the order. Receive what was ordered, then add any extra pieces with **Add stock**. [Full answer](/help/purchase-orders/receive-a-delivery#cannot-receive-more-than-remaining-quantity)
:::

:::faq I made a mistake on a saved order
A saved purchase order can't be edited or cancelled yet. Raise a new, correct order, tell the supplier, and ask ScaleEzy support to cancel the wrong one. [Full answer](/help/purchase-orders/create-a-purchase-order#i-made-a-mistake-on-a-saved-order)
:::

## Offers

:::faq The offer does not come off at the till
Check it says **Running**, then check the minimum spend, the stores it applies to, and whether a higher-priority offer took that item. [Full answer](/help/offers/create-an-offer#the-offer-does-not-come-off-at-the-till)
:::

:::faq A salesperson cannot give a discount at all
Their role doesn't allow discounts by hand. The owner can add it to the role, or a manager can give the discount. [Full answer](/help/offers/till-rules#a-salesperson-cannot-give-a-discount-at-all)
:::

## Your shop settings

:::faq "A team member with this email already exists"
Someone in your shop already uses this email. Use a different email, or find that person in the list. [Full answer](/help/settings/add-team-member#a-team-member-with-this-email-already-exists)
:::

:::faq It will not let me switch a store off
The store still has stock or unfinished purchase orders, and the message says which. Move the stock or finish the orders, then try again. [Full answer](/help/settings/locations#it-will-not-let-me-switch-a-store-off)
:::

:::faq A Shopify sale did not arrive as an order
Open **Waiting orders** in **Settings → Storefront**. The reason is written on the order. Fix it, then press **Retry**. [Full answer](/help/settings/connect-shopify#a-shopify-sale-did-not-arrive-as-an-order)
:::

:::faq Day Book numbers are for the wrong store
Check the location box next to **Previous day**. **All locations** adds up every store and godown. [Full answer](/help/settings/day-book#the-numbers-are-for-the-wrong-store)
:::
