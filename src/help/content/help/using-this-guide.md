This guide works in any web browser, on a computer or a phone. You don't need to sign in to read it, so you can also share a page with someone who has no ScaleEzy account.

## 1. Open the guide

There are two ways in:

- Go to **inventory.scaleezy.com/help**.
- Inside ScaleEzy, click [[1]] the **?** button in the top bar. It opens the guide page for the screen you are on, in a new browser tab, so a half-finished sale or order stays open in the first tab. If a screen has no guide page yet, you land on the Help Center home.

![The top bar of ScaleEzy. The question mark button next to the alerts bell is marked 1.](6-help-button.webp "The ? button is on every screen, next to the bell.")

## 2. Find your way around the guide

![The Help Center on a computer. The menu on the left is marked 1, Search the guide 2, the language button 3, the light or dark button 4 and Sign in 5.](1-the-guide.webp "The same frame is around every guide page.")

- [[1]] **The menu** lists every topic. Click a topic to see its pages. **Help Center home** takes you back to the start.
- [[2]] **Search the guide** finds any page (see the next step).
- [[3]] **The language button** shows the language you are reading in.
- [[4]] **Light or dark** switches the screen colours. It is the same choice as inside ScaleEzy on this device.
- [[5]] **Sign in** opens the ScaleEzy sign-in screen. If you are already signed in, the button says **Open the app** and takes you to your Dashboard.

## 3. Search

1. Click **Search the guide**, or press **Ctrl K** on the keyboard (**⌘ K** on a Mac). The **/** key works too.
2. [[1]] Type what you want to do, in simple words, for example *receive delivery* or *password*.
3. [[2]] Press the **↓** and **↑** keys to choose a result, then press **Enter** to open it. You can also click a result.

![The search box with receive delivery typed in it, marked 1. The second result, Add stock without a purchase order, is chosen and marked 2.](2-search.webp "A result with an arrow opens the page at that exact step or problem.")

Before you type, the box shows a few **Popular** pages. Press **Esc** to close search.

:::tip Nothing found?
Try a shorter or simpler word, like *sale*, *stock* or *password*. Every word you type must be on the page.
:::

## 4. Choose your language

Click the language button at the top and choose **English**, **हिन्दी** (Hindi), **తెలుగు** (Telugu), **தமிழ்** (Tamil) or **ಕನ್ನಡ** (Kannada).

![The language list open under the language button: English is ticked, then Hindi, Telugu, Tamil and Kannada, each in its own script with the English name under it.](3-language.webp "Each language is written in its own script.")

- The guide remembers your choice on this device. Next time it opens in the same language.
- Button and screen names stay in English, the same as on the ScaleEzy screen, so you can match them.
- A page that is not translated yet is shown in English, with a short note at the top saying so.

## 5. Read a page

![A guide page. On this page is marked 1, Try it in your shop 2, a picture 3 and a green number in the steps 4.](4-a-guide-page.webp "Pictures are real screens from a demo shop, not your own data.")

- [[1]] **On this page** lists the parts of the page. Click one to jump to it.
- [[2]] **Try it in your shop** opens that screen in ScaleEzy. It is on pages about one screen. If you are not signed in, it asks you to sign in first.
- [[3]] **Click a picture** to see it larger. Click again, or press **Esc**, to close it.
- [[4]] **Green numbers** in the steps match the green numbered circles in the picture below them. Find the same number in the picture to see where to click.

On a smaller screen, **On this page** is hidden and **Try it in your shop** moves to the top of the page.

Coloured boxes point out things to remember:

- **Tip**: a quicker or easier way.
- **Good to know**: something that helps you understand.
- **Careful**: something that can go wrong or can't be undone.

## 6. Common problems and the next page

![The Common problems part of a page. The first problem is marked 1. The Previous and Next links at the bottom are marked 2.](5-problems-and-next.webp "Every problem opens with a click.")

- [[1]] Most pages end with **Common problems**. Click a problem to open its answer. All of them are also collected on [Common problems](/help/help/common-problems).
- [[2]] **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom take you through the guide in menu order, so you can read a whole topic from start to end.

## On a phone

The menu is hidden to give you more room.

1. Tap [[1]] the **☰** button at the top left to open the menu. It turns into an **✕** to close it.
2. [[2]] The language buttons are at the top of the menu. Tap one to switch.
3. Tap the magnifying glass at the top to search.

![The guide on a phone with the menu open. The close button is marked 1 and the language buttons are marked 2.](7-on-a-phone-phone.webp "Tap outside the menu to close it.")

## Common problems

:::faq The ? button opened the Help Center home, not a page
That screen has no guide page of its own yet. Use **Search the guide** and type the screen's name.
:::

:::faq The guide is in the wrong language
Click the language button at the top (on a phone, open the **☰** menu) and choose your language. The choice is kept per device and browser, so a new phone or browser starts in English.
:::

:::faq A page is in English even though I chose another language
That page is not translated yet. The note at the top of the page says so. The steps and pictures are the same.
:::

:::faq Ctrl K does nothing
Click once on the guide page first, so the keyboard is working in the guide, then press **Ctrl K** again. Or click **Search the guide** at the top.
:::
