## September 2026

### Help Center

This guide. Simple steps with real pictures for every part of ScaleEzy, a search box (**Ctrl K**), and a **?** button in the top bar of the app that opens the guide for the screen you are on.

Read the guide in **हिन्दी** (Hindi), **తెలుగు** (Telugu), **தமிழ்** (Tamil) or **ಕನ್ನಡ** (Kannada) as well as English: choose from the language button at the top of the guide, or from the **☰** menu on a phone. See [Using this guide](/help/help/using-this-guide).

### Racks & shelves

Know exactly which shelf every piece is on. Set up your racks, print shelf labels, scan to put stock away, and find any piece in seconds with **Where is it?**. Counter sales take pieces off the shop floor shelves by themselves. Start with [How shelves work](/help/shelves/how-shelves-work).

### Smaller improvements

- **Inventory** has new **Stock alerts** and **Stock counts** buttons, so both are one click away.
- **Purchase Orders** can be filtered by status, for example *Open (waiting for delivery)*. The **Open PO Value** tile on the Dashboard now opens exactly those orders.
- The Sign in screen explains what to do when you forget your password: press **Forgot your password?**.
- Changing a password, or switching a team member off, now signs that person out on every device. You can also **Sign out other devices** yourself from **Settings → General Info**.

### New sale at the counter

Sell to walk-in customers from a computer or tablet: look up the customer by phone, scan items, give a discount by hand with a reason, take cash, UPI, card or a split payment, and print a receipt. See [Make a counter sale](/help/orders/new-sale).
