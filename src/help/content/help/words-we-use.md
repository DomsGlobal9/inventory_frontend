## Products

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Product** | One thing you sell, for example *Cotton Kurti*. |
| **Variant** | One size and colour of a product, for example *Cotton Kurti, Blue, M*. Stock is counted per variant. |
| **SKU** | The short code of a variant, for example *KUR-COT-BLU-M*. Unique in your shop. |
| **Barcode** | The number printed as lines on the price tag. Scanning it finds the variant. |
| **Department** | Who a product is for: *Women*, *Men*, *Kids* or *Unisex*. It is the **Product Category** you choose when you add a product. An offer can cover, or leave out, whole departments. |
| **Barcode scanner** | A USB or Bluetooth gun that reads a barcode or QR code and types it into the box you clicked, then presses Enter. See [Use a barcode scanner](/help/start/barcode-scanner). |
| **Draft** | A product that is saved but not yet for sale. |
| **Published** | A product that is ready and for sale. |
| **Archived** | A product you no longer sell, kept for its history. |
| **You pay / Cost** | What one piece cost you. Only people allowed to see costs see it. |
| **You sell at** | The selling price. |

## Stock

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Store / Location** | A place that holds stock: a shop, a godown, a warehouse. Pick the one you work in at the top of the screen. |
| **Stock** | How many pieces a store has right now. |
| **Godown** | A store room or warehouse kept as its own store in ScaleEzy. It has its own stock, shelves and alerts. See [Working with more than one store](/help/start/more-than-one-store). |
| **Reorder level** | When stock falls to this number, you get a low stock alert. |
| **Low stock / Out of stock** | At or under the reorder level / none left. |
| **Receive / Stock in** | Stock coming in. Your stock goes up. |
| **Issue / Stock out** | Stock going out that is not a sale: damage, a sample, returned to the supplier. |
| **Adjust** | Correcting the number after you count what is really there. |
| **Ledger** | The full history of every stock change, who did it and when. Nothing in it is ever overwritten. |
| **Stock count (audit)** | Counting everything in a store and correcting the differences in one go. |
| **Transfer** | Moving stock from one of your stores to another. |
| **Dead stock** | Stock that has not sold for a long time. |

## Racks & shelves

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Shelf address** | The name on a shelf, for example *FLOOR-C1-1*: area FLOOR, cupboard C1, shelf 1. |
| **Label** | The sticker on a shelf with its address and a QR code to scan. |
| **Shop floor** | Shelves where customers shop. Counter sales take from these first. |
| **Back room** | Store room or godown shelves, out of customers' sight. |
| **Not shelved** | Pieces that are in the store but not on any shelf yet. Shelves + Not shelved = your stock. |
| **Put away** | Putting pieces on a shelf and scanning the shelf label. |
| **Pick** | Walking the shelves to collect the pieces for orders. |
| **Shelf issue** | Something that did not add up, for example a piece sold from the back room, or a count lower than expected. A manager checks it and marks it resolved. |

## Buying

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Supplier** | Who you buy from. |
| **Purchase order (PO)** | The list of what you order from a supplier, with prices. |
| **Sent** | The purchase order has gone to the supplier and you are waiting for the goods. |
| **Partly received** | Some of the order has arrived, not all. |
| **GRN (Goods Receipt)** | The record of a delivery you received: what came, who checked it, the supplier's invoice number. |

## Selling

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Counter sale** | A sale to a customer standing in your shop, made with **New sale**. |
| **Order** | Any sale: at the counter, online or from Shopify. |
| **Confirmed** | An order you have accepted. Its stock is kept aside for it. |
| **Reserved (held)** | Pieces kept aside for a confirmed order. They are still in the store, but nobody else can sell them. |
| **Dispatch / Send out** | Handing the pieces over or sending them. This is when stock goes down for an online order. |
| **Return** | Pieces a customer brings back. They are checked, then go back into stock or are marked damaged. |
| **Offer** | A discount you set up once, which works at the counter, online and on Shopify. |
| **Customer group** | A name like *VIP* or *Wholesale* that you give to some customers on their page. An offer can be only for some groups. |
| **Discount by hand** | A discount the salesperson gives at the counter, with a reason, up to the limit the owner set. |
| **Day Book** | One day's summary: what came in, what went out, sales and profit. |

## Your team

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Role** | A job, like Sales or Warehouse. It decides what a person can see and do. |
| **Permission** | One thing a role is allowed to do, for example *see what you paid*. |
| **Owner (Super Admin)** | The shop owner. Can do everything, and this role cannot be changed. |
