:::tip Buying from a supplier?
If you raised a purchase order, receive the goods on that order instead: [Receive a delivery](/help/purchase-orders/receive-a-delivery). It updates the order and gives you a receipt.
:::

## 1. Find the item and press +

1. In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Inventory**.
2. On the item's line, click [[2]] **+** (Receive Stock).

![The Inventory screen. Inventory is marked 1 in the menu and the + button on the Chanderi Dupatta line is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Stock is added to the store chosen at the top of the screen.")

## 2. Fill in the form

1. [[1]] **Quantity**: how many pieces came in.
2. [[2]] **Unit Cost (₹)**: what you paid for one piece. For people who can see costs, it starts with the item's usual cost. If you leave it blank, ScaleEzy uses the usual cost. An item that has never had a cost counts as ₹0.
3. [[3]] **Reason**: why the stock came in.
4. [[4]] **Reference No**: a bill or invoice number, so you can find it later. Optional. You can also write a **Note**.

![The Receive Stock form. Quantity is marked 1, Unit Cost 2, Reason 3 and Reference No 4.](2-fill-in.webp "Only Quantity must be filled in.")

The reasons you can pick:

![The Reason list open: PURCHASE, RETURN, INITIAL STOCK, SUPPLIER DELIVERY and PURCHASE RECEIPT.](3-reasons.webp "Use INITIAL STOCK when you first enter what is already in the shop.")

## 3. Press Receive Stock

Click [[1]] **Receive Stock**. You see *Stock added successfully*.

![The Receive Stock button at the bottom of the form is marked 1.](4-press-receive.webp "Cancel closes the form without changing anything.")

## 4. Check it worked

[[1]] The **Stock Qty** on the line goes up by the number you entered.

![The Chanderi Dupatta line. Its Stock Qty is marked 1.](5-check.webp "The change also appears in the item's history.")

:::note The new pieces are not on a shelf yet
If your store uses shelves, the new pieces wait under **Shelves → Put away**. See [Put away stock](/help/shelves/put-away).
:::

## Who can do this

Owners, admins, managers and stock room staff. Salespeople cannot add stock.

## Common problems

:::faq I do not see the + button
Your role is not allowed to bring stock in. Ask your shop owner to give you **Bring stock in by hand** in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
:::

:::faq I added it to the wrong store
Take it out again with [Remove stock](/help/inventory/remove-stock), change the store at the top of the screen, and add it again. Or move it with [Move stock between stores](/help/transfers/transfers).
:::

:::faq I typed the wrong quantity
Do not add it again. Use [Correct stock](/help/inventory/correct-stock) and type the real number of pieces.
:::
