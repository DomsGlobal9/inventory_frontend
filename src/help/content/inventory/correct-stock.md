Use this for **one or two items**. To count the whole store, use [Count your stock](/help/inventory/stock-counts).

## 1. Find the item and press the adjust button

In **Inventory**, click [[1]] the adjust button (Adjust Stock) on the item's line. It is the third button, with two sliders.

![The Inventory screen. The adjust button on the Chanderi Dupatta line is marked 1.](1-open.webp "The count is for the store chosen at the top of the screen.")

## 2. Type what you counted

1. [[1]] **Counted Physical Stock**: the number of pieces you really have. Type the full number, not the difference.
2. [[2]] Check the line under it. *System says* is what ScaleEzy had. *Adjustment will be* is the change it will make, for example **-2**.
3. [[3]] **Reason**: **AUDIT** or **AUDIT CORRECTION** after a count, or **MANUAL ADJUSTMENT** / **MANUAL CORRECTION** to fix a mistake.
4. Write a **Note** so others know why. Optional, but a good habit.

![The Stock Adjustment form. Counted Physical Stock is marked 1, the System says line 2 and Reason 3.](2-type-count.webp "Two pieces fewer than the system says, so the adjustment is -2.")

## 3. Press Apply Adjustment

Click [[1]] **Apply Adjustment**. You see *Stock adjusted successfully* and **Stock Qty** shows your counted number.

![The Apply Adjustment button at the bottom of the form is marked 1.](3-apply.webp "The change is saved in the item's history.")

:::warning This changes your stock and is kept forever
The correction is saved in [Stock history](/help/inventory/stock-history) with your name and cannot be deleted. If you make a mistake, adjust again with the right number.
:::

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and managers. Stock room staff can count the whole store with [Count your stock](/help/inventory/stock-counts) instead.

## Common problems

:::faq Adjustment will be shows 0
The number you typed is the same as what ScaleEzy already has. Nothing needs fixing.
:::

:::faq "Only 2 of this item are free here: 3 are held for orders"
You typed fewer pieces than are held for confirmed orders. Send or cancel those orders first, or count again.
:::

:::faq The number was right, but on the wrong shelf
That is not a stock problem. Use [Move between shelves](/help/shelves/move).
:::
