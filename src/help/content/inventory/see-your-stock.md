## 1. Open Inventory

In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Inventory**. You see every item with its stock in the store chosen at the top of the screen.

![The Inventory Overview screen. Inventory is marked 1 in the left menu.](1-open.webp "Each line is one item: one colour and size of a product.")

:::warning Check the store at the top
The numbers are for **one store**. If a number looks wrong, first check the store name at the top of the screen.
:::

## 2. Find an item

1. [[1]] Type part of the name, the SKU or the barcode. You can also scan the price tag here.
2. [[2]] Show only **Healthy**, **Low Stock**, **Out of Stock** or **Archived** items. **All Inventory** shows everything.
3. [[3]] Change the order, for example **Lowest Stock First** to see what is running out.

![The search box is marked 1, the All Inventory filter 2 and the Recently Updated sort 3.](2-search-and-filter.webp "Stock Qty is how many pieces this store has.")

## 3. Read a line

- **Status** tells you at a glance: **Healthy**, **Low Stock** (at or under its reorder level) or **Out of Stock**.
- **Stock Qty** is the number of pieces in this store.
- **Avg Cost** and **Total Value** show what the stock cost you. Only people allowed to see costs (owners, admins and managers) see these two columns.

## 4. The buttons at the end of each line

- [[1]] **+** Receive Stock: add pieces. See [Add stock](/help/inventory/add-stock).
- [[2]] **−** Issue Stock: take pieces out, for example damaged ones. See [Remove stock](/help/inventory/remove-stock).
- [[3]] Adjust Stock: set the number you really counted. See [Correct stock](/help/inventory/correct-stock).
- [[4]] View Ledger: every change to this item. See [Stock history](/help/inventory/stock-history).

![The four buttons at the end of the Mysore Crepe Saree line, marked 1 to 4.](3-row-buttons.webp "Hold the mouse over a button to see its name.")

Stock room staff see fewer buttons: only **+** and the history button, and no cost columns.

![The same list as stock room staff see it, with only Stock Qty and two buttons on each line.](5-stock-room-view.webp "What Ravi from the stock room sees.")

## 5. More stock screens

At the top right:

- [[1]] **Stock alerts**: items that are low or out of stock. See [Low stock alerts](/help/inventory/low-stock-alerts).
- [[2]] **Stock counts**: count the whole store. See [Count your stock](/help/inventory/stock-counts).
- [[3]] **View Ledger**: every stock change in the shop.

![The Stock alerts button marked 1, Stock counts 2 and View Ledger 3.](4-top-buttons.webp "Stock counts shows only for people allowed to count stock.")

:::tip On a phone
The buttons at the end of each line are off to the right. Swipe the list sideways to reach them.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq An item is missing from the list
Clear the search box and set the filter back to **All Inventory**. Archived products only show under **Archived**.
:::

:::faq The number is different from what is on the shelf
Check the store at the top first. If it is still wrong, count it and use [Correct stock](/help/inventory/correct-stock).
:::

:::faq I cannot see the − and adjust buttons
Your role can only receive stock. Ask your shop owner. Removing and correcting stock needs the **Correct stock counts** permission.
:::
