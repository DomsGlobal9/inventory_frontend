## 1. Open Stock counts

1. In the menu on the left, click **Inventory**.
2. At the top right, click [[1]] **Stock counts**.

![The Inventory screen. The Stock counts button at the top right is marked 1.](1-open.webp "Counts are for the store chosen at the top of the screen.")

## 2. Start a new count, or open one

- [[1]] **New Audit** makes a new count for the store chosen at the top, named after this month, for example *September 2026 Full Count*. It opens straight away.
- [[2]] Or click a count in the list to open it.

![The Inventory Audits list. New Audit is marked 1 and the September full count line is marked 2.](2-list.webp "DRAFT means counting has not started yet.")

The count lists every product you sell, with how many ScaleEzy expects in this store.

## 3. Press Start Counting

Click [[1]] **Start Counting** when you are ready to count. Until then, the boxes are locked.

![A draft count called September full count. The Start Counting button is marked 1.](3-start.webp "10 items to count means 10 lines, one per colour and size.")

:::warning Avoid selling while you count
Sales and deliveries during the count can make the numbers wrong. If you can, count before opening or after closing.
:::

## 4. Scan or type what you count

1. [[1]] Scan an item's barcode. The cursor jumps to that item's count box.
2. [[2]] Type how many pieces you counted, then press **Tab** or click outside the box. It saves by itself.
3. [[3]] **Difference** shows at once: green **0** means it matches, red means more or fewer than expected.

![Counting in progress. The scan box is marked 1, the Counted box of Banarasi Silk Saree 2 and its Difference, -1, is marked 3.](4-type-counts.webp "Leave a box empty if you did not count that item.")

You can stop and come back later. What you typed is kept.

:::tip Watch the small words under a box
*unsaved* means press **Tab** or click outside the box. *saving...* means wait a moment. *not saved - retry* means it did not save: click it to try again.
:::

## 5. Press Complete Audit

When everything is counted, click [[1]] **Complete Audit**. [[2]] The yellow note is the reminder not to sell while counting.

![The top of the count. Complete Audit is marked 1 and the yellow warning is marked 2.](5-complete.webp "Complete Audit is only there while counting is in progress.")

## 6. Confirm

Read the message and click [[1]] **Complete Audit** again.

![The confirm box. It says completing will make automatic stock adjustments and cannot be undone. Complete Audit is marked 1.](6-confirm.webp "Cancel goes back to counting.")

:::warning This changes your stock and cannot be undone
For every item with a difference, ScaleEzy corrects the stock to the number you counted. Items you left empty are not changed. Each correction shows in [Stock history](/help/inventory/stock-history) with the reason AUDIT CORRECTION.
:::

## 7. See the result

The count is now **COMPLETED**. [[1]] **Accuracy** is how many of the counted items matched: 100% means no differences.

![The Inventory Audits list. The count is COMPLETED and its Accuracy is marked 1.](7-done.webp "Green with a tick is 95% or better. Red is under 95%.")

## Who can do this

Owners, admins, managers and stock room staff.

## Common problems

:::faq A count has not been saved yet
A count box did not save. Click into that box, press **Tab**, wait for it to save, then press **Complete Audit** again.
:::

:::faq No item in this audit matches what I scanned
The barcode is not on this count. The item may be archived, or it was added after the count was made. Count it later with [Correct stock](/help/inventory/correct-stock).
:::

:::faq A Changed to number shows under Expected
Stock changed after counting started, for example a sale. When you type a count, ScaleEzy uses the stock at that moment, so earlier sales are not taken off twice. The **Difference** on the screen still compares with the old Expected number, so for that line trust the **Changed to** number instead.
:::
