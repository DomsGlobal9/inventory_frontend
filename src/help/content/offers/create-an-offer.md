Only the owner or an admin can create offers. Other roles do not see **New offer**.

In this example we make **15% off every kurti for two weeks**.

## 1. Click New offer

In the menu on the left, click **Offers**, then [[1]] **New offer**.

![The Offers screen with the New offer button at the top right marked 1.](1-new-offer.webp "The offer form opens on top of the list.")

## 2. The discount

1. [[1]] **Name**: the customer sees this on the receipt, so keep it short and clear.
2. [[2]] **Takes money off**: **Each item** (off the price of each item it covers) or **The whole bill** (off the bill total).
3. [[3]] **How**: **Percentage off**, **Amount off** or **Set a price** (each piece sells at that price, for example 999).
4. [[4]] How much: *15* for 15% off.
5. [[5]] **Never more than (optional)**: a cap in rupees, so a percentage cannot give away too much on a very large bill.

![The discount part. Name Kurti week 15% off is marked 1, Each item or The whole bill 2, How: Percentage off 3, Percent 15 is marked 4 and Never more than 5.](2-the-discount.webp "With Amount off on items you also choose whether it comes off every piece or each line once.")

## 3. Applies to

1. [[1]] Choose what kind of thing it is for: **Products**, **Types of garment**, **Particular items (SKU)** or **Departments**.
2. [[2]] Click each one it covers. The small number is how many products have it.
3. [[3]] **Leave some things out** if a few items should never get the offer.

![Applies to. Types of garment is marked 1, the chosen Kurti chip is marked 2 and Leave some things out 3.](3-applies-to.webp "Choose Particular items (SKU) for one size or colour only.")

An offer on **The whole bill** applies to everything on the bill, so this part only offers **Leave some things out**.

## 4. Who gets it

1. [[1]] **Automatically** (everyone who buys) or **With a code**. For codes see [Offer codes](/help/offers/offer-codes).
2. [[2]] **Customers**: **Everyone**, or **Some groups** of customers only.
3. [[3]] **Minimum spend (optional)** and **Minimum items (optional)**: the bill needs this much before the offer comes off.

![Who gets it. Automatically is marked 1, Customers: Everyone 2 and Minimum spend 3.](4-who-gets-it.webp "Put customers in groups on each customer's page.")

## 5. When

1. [[1]] **Starts**: the date. Leave the time empty to start at midnight.
2. [[2]] **Last day (optional)**: it runs until 11:59 pm that day. Leave it empty to run until you stop it.
3. [[3]] Tick **Only at certain hours** for a happy hour: choose the days and the from and to times.

![When. The start date 17-09-2026 is marked 1, the last day 01-10-2026 is marked 2 and Only at certain hours 3.](5-when.webp "Times are in your shop's own time.")

## 6. More options (if you need them)

Click **More options** to open them.

1. [[1]] **Sells at**: **Till**, **Online store** or both. Online includes the website and Shopify. Under it, **Locations** limits the offer to some stores.
2. [[2]] **Uses per customer**: only bills with a customer on them can be counted, so walk-in guests will not get an offer with this limit.
3. [[3]] **Total uses**: for example 50 for the first 50 customers.
4. [[4]] **Priority**: when two offers want the same item, the higher number wins.
5. [[5]] **Can combine with other offers**: leave it off and only the best single offer applies.

![More options. Sells at Till and Online store is marked 1, Uses per customer 2, Total uses 3, Priority 4 and Can combine with other offers 5.](6-more-options.webp "Leave these as they are for a simple offer.")

## 7. Check the sentence, then Create offer

At the bottom, [[1]] a sentence says in plain words what the offer will do. Read it. If it is right, press [[2]] **Create offer**.

![The sentence 15% off every Kurti, until 1 Oct 2026 is marked 1 and Create offer is marked 2.](7-check-and-create.webp "If the sentence is not what you meant, change the form above. Nothing is saved yet.")

## 8. Start it

A new offer is always saved as a [[1]] **Draft**, so nothing changes at the till by accident. Press [[2]] the start button (a triangle) on its row.

![The new offer Kurti week 15% off in the list, with Draft marked 1 and the start button marked 2.](8-start-it.webp "If the start date is in the future, the button shows a calendar instead and the offer says Starts later.")

## 9. Check it is running

The status changes to [[1]] **Running**. The offer now comes off at the till and online.

![The same offer, now with the status Running marked 1.](9-running.webp "The Used column counts the orders that got it.")

## Common problems

:::faq Create offer is greyed out
Something is missing. Read the sentence at the bottom of the form, just above the button, for example *Say when the offer starts*. Also check that **Sells at** has at least one box ticked, **Only at certain hours** has a day chosen, and **Some groups** has a group chosen.
:::

:::faq The offer does not come off at the till
Check that it says **Running**, not Draft, Paused or Starts later. Then check the minimum spend, the stores under **More options**, and whether another offer with a higher priority took that item.
:::

:::faq "This offer already ended. Change its dates before starting it."
The last day is in the past. Click **Edit**, change **Last day**, save, then start it.
:::

:::faq I don't see New offer
Only the owner or an admin can create offers. Everyone else can see them.
:::
