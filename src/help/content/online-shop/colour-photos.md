A saree you sell in five colours usually gets photographed in one. On the shop, the other four then show the first colour's photograph, and a customer choosing **Bottle Green** looks at a red saree.

This makes the missing ones. It takes the **front view** of the colour you did photograph and puts the same piece in the other colours. The weave, the border, the blouse and the model stay exactly as they are — only the colour of the cloth changes.

## 1. Open the product's Images tab

Open the product from **Products**, then click [[1]] the **Images** tab.

![A product open on the Images tab, marked 1, with the photographs of its first colour and The colours with no photograph card underneath.](1-images-tab.webp "The card only appears when at least one colour already has a front view and another colour has nothing.")

## 2. Make the missing colours

The card [[1]] **The colours with no photograph** names exactly which colours it will make. Read that line — it is the whole of what will happen.

Click [[2]] **MAKE THESE 2 COLOURS** (the button says how many).

![The colours with no photograph card, with the line naming the colours marked 1 and the MAKE THESE COLOURS button marked 2.](2-make-these-colours.webp "About a minute for each colour. You can leave the screen open and watch it.")

While it runs you see **Making the Bottle Green one…** and how many are done. [[1]] **Stop** stops it after the colour it is on; anything already made is kept.

## 3. Check them before your customers do

When it finishes you see **Made: Bottle Green, Indigo.** and the new photographs appear in the gallery against those colours.

![The gallery after the colours were made: four photographs for Bottle Green — front, left, right and back.](3-done.webp "Four views per colour, the same four the first colour has.")

:::warning Look at every one
These are made by a machine from your photograph. Most come out right; some do not. Look at each one before you leave the screen, and delete any that is wrong — a wrong photograph on your shop is worse than none at all, because the customer only finds out when the parcel arrives.
:::

Photograph any colour yourself instead if you would rather. A real photograph always wins, and you can upload one over these at any time.

## 4. On the shop

Each colour now shows its own photographs. Choosing a colour changes the pictures.

![The shop page with Bottle Green chosen and its own four photographs in the row underneath.](4-on-the-shop.webp "A colour with no photographs of its own still falls back to the product's first one, so nothing is ever blank.")

## While adding a new product

The same thing is offered in the **Add a product** wizard, on the photos step, right after the first colour is generated — so a new product can be finished in one sitting. See [Photos](/help/products/photos).

## Common problems

:::faq The card does not appear
It needs two things: one colour with a **front view** photograph, and at least one other colour with no photographs at all. A colour that already has a picture is left alone.
:::

:::faq A colour came out wrong
Delete those photographs from the gallery and either run it again or photograph that colour yourself. Running it again gives a different result each time.
:::

:::faq It stopped with a message about the limit
Making colours uses the same allowance as try-on. Wait, or ask for more, and run it again — the colours already made are kept.
:::

:::faq The colour on the photograph is not quite my colour
It uses the colour swatch set against that colour in **Settings → Catalog Configuration → Colors**. If the swatch is wrong, the photograph will be too. Check the swatch first.
:::
