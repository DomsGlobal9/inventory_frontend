Nobody sets the filters on your shop. They are built from what is on your products, and they appear on their own once there is something to choose between.

## Where each filter comes from

| Filter on the shop | Field on the product |
|---|---|
| **Fabric** | **Material / Fabric** |
| **Made** | **Design / Craft** |
| The pills across the top | **Dress Type** (Saree, Kurti, Dupatta…) |
| **Price** | The selling price |

![Add a product, with Design / Craft marked 1 and Material / Fabric marked 2.](1-product-fields.webp "These two boxes are the whole of what the Fabric and Made filters are made of.")

The choices in those two boxes come from **Settings → Catalog Configuration** — **Design Types** and **Materials**. Keep that list tidy and your filters stay tidy.

## What a customer sees

They tap **Filters** and see each group with a count beside every choice.

![The filters open on the shop: Fabric with Cotton 3, Silk 2, and Made with Embroidered 3, Chikankari 1, Plain 1.](2-filters-on-the-shop.webp "Tapping Chikankari shows the one piece. Tapping it again clears it.")

:::note A filter with one choice does not appear
If every product you sell is Silk, the **Fabric** group is hidden — there would be nothing to narrow. The group appears as soon as a second fabric exists on a published piece.
:::

## Getting this right pays off twice

The same fields also show in **Details** on each piece — *Fabric: Cotton · Made: Embroidered* — which is often what a customer actually wants to know before asking you anything.

:::tip Be consistent, not detailed
`Chikankari` on forty products is worth far more than `Chikankari work`, `chikan`, and `Chikankari (Lucknow)` on thirteen each. Every spelling becomes its own filter with its own small count, and the list gets useless. Pick the word, add it once in **Catalog Configuration**, and always choose it from the list.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq My new craft is not in the dropdown
Add it in **Settings → Catalog Configuration → Design Types** and it appears in the box. See [Catalog settings](/help/settings/catalog-settings).
:::

:::faq The Made filter is not on my shop
Either no published product has **Design / Craft** filled in, or they all have the same one. Fill in a second and it appears.
:::

:::faq The counts look wrong
They count only what a customer can see: published pieces, in the stores you sell online from, and not hidden by **Hide pieces that are sold out**.
:::
