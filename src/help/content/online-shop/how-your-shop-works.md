Your online shop is a real shop page on the web, at an address of your own, made from the products that are already in ScaleEzy. You do not upload anything twice: the photos, prices, colours and sizes on it are the ones your staff already put in.

## What a customer sees

They open your link on their phone. They see your shop's name and logo, your banners, and your pieces — the ones you have published, in the stores you chose. They can search, filter by fabric or craft, open a piece, look at every photograph of it, and pick a colour and a size.

![The Lakshmi Silks shop: the shop name at the top, a Festival collection banner, the filter buttons, and a grid of six pieces.](1-your-shop.webp "This is the whole shop. There is nothing for the customer to install and nothing to sign into.")

Two things can happen next, and **you decide which**:

- **Looking only.** The customer reads about the piece and messages you on WhatsApp. Many shops prefer this, because the conversation is where they sell.
- **Ordering.** The customer puts pieces in a bag and orders them. The order lands in **Orders** with the stock already held for it.

See [Taking orders](/help/online-shop/taking-orders) for how to turn ordering on and off.

![A saree open in the shop: the big photograph, a row of small photographs under it, the price, the colours, and Add to bag and Buy now.](2-a-piece.webp "The small photographs under the big one scroll sideways with a finger.")

## What it is made from

| On the shop page | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Shop name, logo, address, GSTIN | **Settings → General Info** |
| The pieces, their photos, colours and sizes | **Products** |
| Prices and offers | The same ones your till uses |
| Whether a piece shows at all | Whether it is published, and which stores you chose |
| Fabric, craft and type filters | The fields on each product |

:::note Nothing is shown until you open it
Choosing an address and filling in the settings does not put anything on the web. Nothing is visible to anybody until you press **Open my shop**.
:::

## What it does not do yet

- **Paying online** is not available yet. Customers pay when the order arrives, in cash or by UPI. The **Online** payment box is there but switched off.
- **Delivery tracking** is not built in. You send the order the way you send any other.

## Common problems

:::faq I cannot find Online shop in Settings
Only someone with the **Online shop** permission sees that tab. Ask your owner or admin, or see [Roles and permissions](/help/settings/roles).
:::

:::faq Is this the same as connecting my own website?
No. **Storefront** in Settings connects a website you already have, so it can be told about your stock. The online shop is a shop page we host for you, and you need no website of your own. See [Connect your website](/help/settings/connect-website).
:::
