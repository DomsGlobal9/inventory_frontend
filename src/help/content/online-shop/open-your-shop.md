Only someone with the **Online shop** permission can do this. Take a minute over the address: once your shop has been open on one, it is yours for good and cannot be swapped.

## 1. Open the Online shop settings

In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Settings**. In the list of tabs, click [[2]] **Online shop**.

![The Settings screen with Settings marked 1 in the left menu and the Online shop tab marked 2.](1-open.webp "Everything about your shop is on this one screen, top to bottom.")

## 2. Claim your web address

[[1]] Type the name you want in the address box — letters, numbers and hyphens, like `lakshmi-silks`. Then click [[2]] **Claim it**.

![Your shop's web address, with the address box marked 1 and the Claim it button marked 2.](2-the-address.webp "Type it however you like. Lakshmi Silks is tidied into lakshmi-silks when it is saved.")

Once it is claimed, the full address appears underneath with a **Copy** button.

:::warning Choose it carefully
While the shop is open, the address cannot be changed, because customers may have saved it or scanned it from a poster. You can change it before you open the shop, and only then.
:::

## 3. Set the name and which stores sell online

[[1]] **Shop name** is the name customers see at the top. Leave it empty to use your shop's name from **General Info**.

[[2]] **Which stores sell online** decides where the stock comes from. Tick the stores whose stock you are willing to send. A piece only appears if it has stock in one of the ticked stores.

![What customers see, with Shop name marked 1 and the list of stores marked 2.](3-name-and-stores.webp "A shop with one store ticks that one and never thinks about it again.")

## 4. Fill in your seller details

The law asks for your details and someone to contact on every page. The shop will not open without them. See [Your details and your return policy](/help/online-shop/your-details-and-returns).

## 5. Save, then open the shop

Click [[1]] **Save**. Then, at the bottom, click [[2]] **Open my shop**.

![The Save button marked 1 and the Open my shop button marked 2.](4-open-my-shop.webp "Nothing is shown to anybody until you press Open my shop.")

The heading changes to **Your shop is open**, and the **Share your shop** card appears with your link and a printable code.

:::tip Closing it again
**Close the shop** tells anyone who opens the link that it is shut. It deletes nothing — your products, photos and settings all stay, and opening it again puts everything back as it was.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq It says "Before it can open, it needs…"
That line names exactly what is missing — usually the contact details or the return policy. Fill those in, press **Save**, and the **Open my shop** button appears.
:::

:::faq The address box is greyed out
Your shop is already open. Close it first if you really must change the address, and remember that any link or printed code already out there will stop working.
:::

:::faq My pieces are not showing on the shop
Check three things: the product is **published**, it has stock in one of the stores you ticked, and — if it is sold out — whether **Hide pieces that are sold out** is on. See [What customers see](/help/online-shop/what-customers-see).
:::
