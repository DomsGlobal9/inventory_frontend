You do not have to keep the shop page open and watch it. Every online order announces itself inside ScaleEzy.

## 1. The bell rings

A new order raises an alert. The bell at the top right shows a count, and the alert reads **ONLINE SHOP**.

![The bell open at the top of the screen, with an ONLINE SHOP alert reading New online order ORD-1043, marked 1.](1-the-bell.webp "New online order ORD-1043 — Priya ordered 2 pieces for ₹6,400 · 98480 22338. The stock is held for them.")

It is blue, not red. Nothing is wrong: it is something to do, and doing it is how you get paid.

## 2. Or find them in the Alert Centre

Click **See all** on the bell, or open the **Alert Centre**. There is a [[1]] **Your shop** tab that holds these and nothing else, so a busy day of stock warnings never buries an order.

![The Alert Centre with the Your shop tab marked 1 and two online shop alerts listed under it.](2-alert-centre.webp "Stock alerts have their own tabs. This tab is only your shop.")

## 3. Pack and send it from Orders

The order is a normal order in **Orders**, with the customer's name, phone and address, and the pieces with their colours and sizes.

![An online order open in Orders: the customer, the address, the lines with colour and size, and the total.](3-the-order.webp "The stock was held the moment the order was placed, so nobody can sell the same saree at the counter.")

See [Send out online orders](/help/orders/send-online-orders) for packing and marking it sent.

## 4. If a customer cancels

A customer can call off their own order from the shop. When they do, you get a second alert:

> **Order ORD-1043 was cancelled** — Priya called off their online order for ₹6,400. Do not send it. The stock is back on the shelf.

The original **New online order** alert is closed at the same time, so the Alert Centre stops asking someone to pack an order that no longer exists. This matters most when the parcel is half wrapped.

:::note Nothing is sent to your customers by us
These alerts are for you, inside the app. The customer gets their own confirmation on the shop. Nothing here messages anybody on your behalf.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq I did not get an alert for an order
The order always stands even if the alert fails. Check **Orders** — the order is the truth, the alert is only the tap on the shoulder. Also check you have permission to see online shop alerts.
:::

:::faq The alert is still there after I sent the order
Alerts do not close themselves when you pack. Tick it off in the Alert Centre once it is out of the door.
:::

:::faq Can I get these on WhatsApp instead?
Not today. The bell and the Alert Centre are where this shop already looks for things that need doing, so that is where these go.
:::
