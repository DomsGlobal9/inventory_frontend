Banners are the pictures on your shop. You can have up to **six**, and choose **where** each one shows and **on which days**. The **announcement bar** is one short line of words across the top of every page of your shop. A shop with neither simply starts at its products, which is a perfectly good shop.

The **Banners** card only appears once you have claimed a web address — before that there is no shop for a banner to sit on.

## 1. Find the Banners card

In **Settings → Online shop**, scroll down to [[1]] **Banners**. The **Top announcement bar** card is just above it.

![The Banners card in Online shop settings, with Banners marked 1.](1-banners.webp "The card sits between Taking orders and Your details on the shop.")

## 2. Choose a picture

Click **Add a banner** and pick a picture from your computer or phone.

Every banner is one shape: **3 times as wide as it is tall**. **1920 × 640 pixels** is best; it must be at least 1200 pixels wide. A picture of any other shape is refused when you pick it, so the whole banner shows on every phone and laptop.

:::warning iPhone photos
If the picture is an iPhone **HEIC** photo it cannot be read. Share it as a JPEG, or set the camera to **Most Compatible**, and choose it again. The largest a picture can be is 15 MB.
:::

## 3. Words, where it goes, where it shows and when

![Adding a banner: Big words marked 1, Small words 2, When tapped 3, Show on 4, the Show from and Show until dates 5, and Add this banner 6.](2-add-a-banner.webp "Type the words here rather than writing them into the picture — typed words stay readable on a small phone.")

[[1]] **Big words** is the headline, like *Festival collection*. [[2]] **Small words** is the line under it, like *New silks just in*. Both are optional.

[[3]] **When tapped** decides what happens when a customer taps the banner:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Nothing happens** | The banner is just a picture |
| **Search for something** | Opens your shop searched for a word, like `silk` |
| **Open one product** | Opens one piece. Type its code, like `SAR-0012` |

[[4]] **Show on** is where the banner appears. Tick one or more:

| Place | Where customers see it |
|---|---|
| **Home: top slider** | The swiping pictures at the top of your shop's home page |
| **Home: between products** | A wide picture after the first row or two of products. Not shown inside a search or filtered results |
| **Product page** | Under the details of **every** product |

A banner with nothing ticked stays in your list but is not shown anywhere.

[[5]] **Show from** and **Show until** are optional. Set them and the banner switches itself on and off, for example a festival sale from 25 Oct until 5 Nov. Leave them empty and it shows until you hide it.

Click [[6]] **Add this banner**.

Each banner in the list says what customers see right now: **Showing now**, **Starts 10 Nov**, **Ended**, **Hidden**, or **Not placed anywhere**.

## 4. Put them in order

Banners show in the order they are listed. Use the up and down arrows on each row to move it. **Hide it** takes one off the shop for now; **Remove** deletes it, after asking.

## 5. The announcement bar

One short line across the top of every page of your shop, in your shop's colour, like *Diwali sale: 10% off everything till 5 Nov*.

![The Top announcement bar card: the words marked 1, the Show from and Show until dates 2, and Show it on the shop 3.](4-announcement-bar.webp "No picture: it loads at once and reads on the smallest phone.")

1. [[1]] Type the **words**: up to 90 letters, so it fits on one line of a phone.
2. Choose **When tapped**, the same as for a banner.
3. [[2]] Set **Show from** and **Show until** if it is for a few days only.
4. Tick [[3]] **Show it on the shop** and click **Save**.

![The shop with the announcement bar across the top in the shop's colour, and a banner in the top slider below it.](3-on-the-shop.webp "The bar is on every page of the shop: the home page, a product, the bag and the checkout.")

## Common problems

:::faq The Add a banner button is greyed out
You already have six, which is the most a shop can have. Remove one to add another.
:::

:::faq My banner is not on the shop
Look at what the list says beside it. **Starts …** means its first day has not come yet; **Ended** means its last day has passed; **Not placed anywhere** means no place is ticked under **Show on**; **Hidden** means it was hidden.
:::

:::faq The banner between the products does not show
It shows on the home page with nothing searched or filtered, once there are more than four products. Inside a search or a filter it is left out on purpose.
:::

:::faq The words are hard to read on the picture
Choose a picture with a plain, darker area where the words sit — the sky, a wall, a blurred background. Words typed on a busy pattern are hard to read whatever we do behind them.
:::

:::faq My picture was refused
It is not the banner shape. Crop it to 3 times as wide as it is tall — 1920 × 640 pixels — and choose it again. A banner added before this rule shows a note in the list: replace it with a 1920 × 640 picture so it fills the banner.
:::
