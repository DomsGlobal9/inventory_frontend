Banners are the pictures across the top of your shop. Customers swipe through them. You can have up to **six**. A shop with no banners simply starts at its products, which is a perfectly good shop.

The **Banners** card only appears once you have claimed a web address — before that there is no shop for a banner to sit on.

## 1. Find the Banners card

In **Settings → Online shop**, scroll down to [[1]] **Banners**.

![The Banners card in Online shop settings, marked 1, with the Add a banner button.](1-banners.webp "The card sits between Taking orders and Your details on the shop.")

## 2. Choose a picture

Click [[1]] **Add a banner** and pick a picture from your computer or phone.

Use a **wide** photograph — the shape is like a television, not a portrait. A portrait photo will be cut down to a strip and you will lose the top and bottom of it.

:::warning iPhone photos
If the picture is an iPhone **HEIC** photo it cannot be read. Share it as a JPEG, or set the camera to **Most Compatible**, and choose it again. The largest a picture can be is 15 MB.
:::

## 3. Put the words on

[[1]] **Big words** is the headline, like *Festival collection*. [[2]] **Small words** is the line under it, like *New silks just in*. Both are optional.

![Adding a banner: the chosen picture on the left, Big words marked 1, Small words marked 2, When tapped marked 3, and Add this banner marked 4.](2-add-a-banner.webp "Type the words here rather than writing them into the picture — typed words stay readable on a small phone.")

[[3]] **When tapped** decides what happens when a customer taps the banner:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Nothing happens** | The banner is just a picture |
| **Search for something** | Opens your shop searched for a word, like `silk` |
| **Open one product** | Opens one piece. Type its code, like `SAR-0012` |

Click [[4]] **Add this banner**.

## 4. Put them in order

Banners show in the order they are listed. Use the up and down arrows on each row to move it. **Remove** deletes one, after asking.

![The shop on a phone with two banners at the top and the dots showing which one is on screen.](3-on-the-shop.webp "The dots under the picture tell a customer there is more than one to swipe through.")

## Common problems

:::faq The Add a banner button is greyed out
You already have six, which is the most a shop can have. Remove one to add another.
:::

:::faq The words are hard to read on the picture
Choose a picture with a plain, darker area where the words sit — the sky, a wall, a blurred background. Words typed on a busy pattern are hard to read whatever we do behind them.
:::

:::faq The banner is cut off
It is a wide shape. A tall photo loses its top and bottom. Crop it wide before you upload it.
:::
