With **Taking orders** off, customers can look at your shop and ask you on WhatsApp — which is how many shops prefer to sell. With it on, they can put pieces in a bag and order them outright, and the order lands in **Orders** with the stock already held for it.

## 1. Turn ordering on

In **Settings → Online shop**, find the **Taking orders** card and tick [[1]] **Let customers order from my shop**.

![The Taking orders card with Let customers order from my shop marked 1.](1-taking-orders.webp "Your prices and your offers apply automatically — the same ones your till uses.")

More settings appear underneath as soon as it is ticked.

:::warning This holds real stock
An order made on your shop holds the pieces for itself immediately, so the same saree cannot be sold again at the counter. That is the point of it, but it means a shop that cannot actually send parcels should leave this off.
:::

## 2. How customers may pay

[[1]] **When it arrives (cash or UPI)** is the only way to pay today. The customer pays you when the parcel reaches them.

[[2]] **Online (coming with payments)** is greyed out. Paying online needs your payment gateway set up first, and that is not built yet.

![How customers may pay, with When it arrives marked 1 and the greyed-out Online box marked 2.](2-how-they-pay.webp "Leave When it arrives ticked. With neither ticked, nobody can finish an order.")

## 3. Where you deliver

[[1]] Type the PIN codes you deliver to, separated by commas — for example `500029, 500034`. **Leave it empty and you deliver everywhere.**

![Where you deliver with a list of PIN codes typed in, marked 1, and the delivery charge boxes below.](3-where-you-deliver.webp "A customer can check their own PIN code on the product page before they even open the bag.")

A customer outside your list is told at the checkout — **before** they have typed out an address — and pointed at your WhatsApp. Nobody fills in a long form only to be refused at the end.

## 4. Charges and the smallest order

| Box | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Delivery charge** | What you add for delivery. `0` means delivery is always free. |
| **Free delivery above** | Above this order value, delivery costs nothing. Customers are told how much more to add. Leave empty for none. |
| **Smallest order you will send** | Below this, the customer cannot finish. Counted on the pieces, not the delivery. Leave empty for none. |

Press **Save** when you are done.

## 5. What the customer then does

They tap **Add to bag** or **Buy now**, open the bag, and fill in their name, phone and address. They see your delivery charge and your total before they confirm.

![The checkout: the pieces with their colours and sizes, the delivery charge, the total, and the Place the order button.](4-checkout.webp "The colour and the size are written on every line, so nobody orders the wrong one by mistake.")

The order arrives in **Orders** and the bell rings in the app. See [Orders and alerts from your shop](/help/online-shop/orders-and-alerts).

## Common problems

:::faq A customer says they cannot order
Check, in this order: is the shop open; is **Let customers order from my shop** ticked; is **When it arrives** ticked; is their PIN code in your list; is their order above your smallest order.
:::

:::faq The stock went down but I have not sent anything
That is the hold. The pieces are kept for that order so they cannot be sold twice. Cancelling the order puts them back.
:::

:::faq I want to stop taking orders for a while
Untick **Let customers order from my shop** and press **Save**. Your shop stays open and customers can still look and message you. Orders already placed are not affected.
:::
