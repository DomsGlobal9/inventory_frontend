These four switches are on the **What customers see** card in **Settings → Online shop**. Each one applies to your whole shop at once, not to one product.

![The What customers see card with all four switches: Show every photo of a product marked 1, Let customers see it on themselves marked 2, Hide pieces that are sold out marked 3, and Say when a piece is nearly gone marked 4.](1-what-customers-see.webp "Change one, press Save, then open your shop link and look. That is the quickest way to see what each does.")

## 1. Show every photo of a product

**Off** (the usual choice) — customers see only your finished photographs.

**On** — customers see all of them, including the flat-lay photos that Try-On made the others from. A flat-lay is the picture of the cloth laid out flat. Some shops want it shown, because it tells a customer what the fabric really looks like; most would rather show only the model photographs.

## 2. Let customers see it on themselves

Puts a **See it on you** button on every piece that has a photograph. The customer sends their own photo and sees the saree on themselves — the same try-on your staff use from a tag in the shop.

![A saree in the shop with the See it on you button under Add to bag, marked 1.](2-see-it-on-you.webp "The customer's photograph is deleted straight after. It is never stored and never shown to you.")

:::warning Each try-on spends one from your allowance
Every customer who uses it uses one try-on from the same allowance your shop uses. A busy weekend can spend a lot of them. If the switch is greyed out, try-on is not switched on for this deployment at all.
:::

## 3. Hide pieces that are sold out

**On** — a sold-out piece disappears from the shop.

**Off** — it still shows, greyed, so customers can see the range and ask you about it. Leaving it off is worth considering: a customer who sees a sold-out saree they love can tell you they want it, and that is a sale you would otherwise never have heard about. See [Who is waiting](/help/online-shop/who-is-waiting).

## 4. Say when a piece is nearly gone

**On** — a customer sees **Only 3 left** once five or fewer remain, and nothing at all above that.

![A saree in the shop showing Only 2 left under the price, marked 1.](3-few-left.webp "Above five, nothing is shown — so your stock numbers stay yours.")

This is deliberately limited. It never prints your real stock number, and it never says anything while you are comfortably stocked, so a competitor reading your shop learns nothing about how much you hold.

## Common problems

:::faq I turned a switch on and the shop looks the same
Press **Save** first. Then reload the shop page in your browser — an already-open page keeps showing what it loaded.
:::

:::faq A piece shows the wrong photograph for the colour
Each colour shows its own photographs. If a colour has none, it falls back to the product's first photograph. See [Photos for the other colours](/help/online-shop/colour-photos).
:::

:::faq See it on you is not appearing on a piece
It only appears on pieces that have at least one photograph.
:::
