A customer who wanted a piece that had gone is the one customer a shop never hears about. They arrived, they wanted it, they left.

Your shop now lets them leave a number, and it lands on the screen you open first.

## 1. What the customer does

On a sold-out piece they see **Tell the shop you want this**. They type their number, and their name if they like.

![A sold-out saree in the shop with the Tell the shop you want this button, marked 1, and the small form it opens.](1-tell-the-shop.webp "The wording promises only that the shop will get in touch — never that a message will arrive when stock returns.")

:::warning Sold-out pieces must be visible for this to work
If **Hide pieces that are sold out** is on, nobody ever sees the sold-out piece, so nobody can ask for it. See [What customers see](/help/online-shop/what-customers-see).
:::

## 2. What you see

On your **Dashboard**, a card appears: **Waiting for something you had sold out of**. Each line has the customer's name, their phone, the piece, and the day they asked.

![The Dashboard card listing three customers waiting, with a phone number marked 1 and the Done button marked 2.](2-the-widget.webp "The card is not there at all when nobody is waiting. A tile that says 0 every day is a tile people stop seeing.")

- [[1]] Tap the phone number on a phone and it dials.
- [[2]] **Done** takes the line off the list once you have dealt with it.

Only someone with the **Online shop** permission sees this card — these are customers' phone numbers.

## 3. Nothing happens on its own

**No message is ever sent when stock comes back.** There is no watcher, and a button promising one would be a lie told to a customer who then waits and hears nothing.

You see the number and you ring it. For a saree shop — where one piece is often literally one piece — that is the better call anyway: you can offer the nearest thing you do have, which no automatic message could.

:::tip What to say when you ring
You have the piece they wanted and the day they asked. If it is not coming back, that is still a call worth making — you know exactly what they liked, and a shop that rings back is remembered.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq The card is not on my Dashboard
It is hidden when nobody is waiting, which is the usual reason. It is also hidden from anyone without the **Online shop** permission.
:::

:::faq The same customer is listed twice
They are not — asking twice for the same piece keeps one line. Two lines means two different pieces.
:::

:::faq Someone asked about a piece that is back in stock now
Ring them anyway. Nothing told them it had returned.
:::
