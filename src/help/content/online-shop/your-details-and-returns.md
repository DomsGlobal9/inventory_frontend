You are the seller on your online shop, not us. So the law asks for your details and someone a customer can contact on every page — the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020**. Your shop will not open until these are filled in.

Your **name, address and GSTIN** come from **Settings → General Info**. You do not type them again here.

## 1. Who a customer contacts

In **Settings → Online shop**, find the **Your details on the shop** card.

![Your details on the shop, with Who a customer contacts marked 1, Their phone marked 2, Their email marked 3 and the return policy box marked 4.](1-your-details.webp "One real person, one phone they answer. This is what a customer sees when something goes wrong.")

- [[1]] **Who a customer contacts** — a real person's name, not "Customer Care".
- [[2]] **Their phone** — a number that is actually answered.
- [[3]] **Their email** — one that is actually read.

## 2. Your return policy, in your own words

[[4]] Write what you really do, plainly. For example:

> Unworn pieces with the tag on can be returned within 7 days, with the bill.

Write your real rule, not a borrowed one. A customer who reads "30 days, no questions" and then hears "7 days, with the bill" is a complaint you did not need.

Press **Save**.

## 3. Where it appears

All of it shows in the footer of every page of your shop, with your shop's name, address and GSTIN.

![The footer of the shop page: the shop name and address, the GSTIN, the contact person and phone, and the return policy.](2-on-the-shop-page.webp "The same footer is on the shop front, on every piece and at the checkout.")

:::note This is not legal advice
We ask for these because the rules require them and the shop cannot open without them. What your return policy should say is your decision, and if you are unsure, ask someone who advises you on such things.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq My shop will not open and it mentions these
The line under **Open your shop** names exactly what is missing. Fill it in, press **Save**, and the **Open my shop** button appears.
:::

:::faq My GSTIN is wrong on the shop
It comes from **Settings → General Info**, not from this card. Change it there. See [Your shop's details](/help/settings/shop-details).
:::

:::faq Can I leave the return policy empty?
No. It is one of the things the shop will not open without. If you genuinely take no returns, say exactly that — that is a policy too.
:::
