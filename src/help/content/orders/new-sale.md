:::note Use a computer or a tablet
New sale works on the shop's computer or a tablet. It does not open on a phone.
:::

## 1. Open New sale

1. In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Orders**.
2. At the top right, click [[2]] **New sale**.

![The Sales Orders screen. Orders is marked 1 in the left menu and the New sale button is marked 2.](1-open.webp "New sale sells from the store chosen at the top of the screen.")

:::tip Check the store first
The sale takes stock from the store shown at the top of the screen, for example **Main Store (MAIN)**. If it is wrong, change it before you start.
:::

## 2. Type the customer's phone number

1. [[1]] Type the customer's mobile number, for example **98480 22338**.
2. If they have bought before, their name appears under the box. Check it is the right person.
3. Wrong person? Click [[2]] **Not them?** and type the number again.

![The Customer box. The phone number is marked 1. Lakshmi Narayanan is found, with the Not them? button marked 2.](2-customer-phone.webp "The line under the name shows the customer code, the phone and groups such as VIP.")

Every sale needs a phone number. That is how the customer is found next time.

## 3. New customer? Add their name

If nobody has that number yet, you see *New customer — saved when the sale is complete.*

1. [[1]] Type their **Name**.
2. [[2]] Type their email if they want one. You can leave it empty.

![A new phone number. The Name box is marked 1 and the Email (optional) box is marked 2.](3-new-customer.webp "The new customer is saved only when you complete the sale.")

## 4. Scan or search for each item

1. [[1]] **Scan the barcode** on the price tag. The item goes straight into the bill.
2. No scanner? Type a name, SKU or colour, then click the item in the list.

Each item in the list shows:

- [[2]] **Which shelf it is on**, and how many are there. Green shelves are on the shop floor. Orange shelves are in the back room.
- [[3]] **How many this store has.**

![The item search with kanchi typed. The search box is marked 1, the shelf FLOOR-C1-1 under the Maroon saree is marked 2 and 6 here is marked 3.](4-find-items.webp "Scan the same tag again to add one more.")

## 5. Change how many

[[1]] Use **−** and **+** to change the number of pieces.

You cannot sell more than the store has. When you reach that number you see [[2]] **Only 3 here** (the number changes with your stock).

![Two sarees in the bill. The minus and plus buttons of Mysore Crepe Saree are marked 1 and the words Only 3 here are marked 2.](5-how-many.webp "Offers that are running come off by themselves, like Festive 10% off silk here.")

To take an item out of the bill, click the bin button at the end of its line.

## 6. Take money off by hand (only if needed)

A mark on the saree, a regular customer? Click [[1]] the **%** button on that item's line. Its name is **Discount by hand**.

![The item lines. The % button on the Kanchipuram Silk Saree line is marked 1.](6-discount-button.webp "You only see the % button if your role may give discounts by hand.")

## 7. Say how much and why

In the new line [[1]]:

1. Choose **₹** for an amount or **%** for a percent.
2. Type how much comes off.
3. Type **why**, for example *Small mark on the border*. You cannot complete the sale without a reason.

[[2]] The line then shows how much came off by hand.

![The discount line under Kanchipuram Silk Saree is marked 1, with 200 and the reason Small mark on the border. The words minus 200 by hand are marked 2.](7-discount-by-hand.webp "The reason is saved on the order, with your name.")

To take money off the whole bill instead, click **Discount on the bill** on the right.

:::warning Your shop may set a limit
The owner can set the most a cashier may take off by hand, in **Offers → Till rules**. If you go over it, the sale is refused and a manager has to give that discount. See [Till rules](/help/offers/till-rules).
:::

## 8. Check the bill

On the right you see the bill:

1. [[1]] If the customer has an **offer code**, type it and click **Apply**. If the code does not work, the reason shows next to it.
2. [[2]] Offers that are running come off by themselves.
3. [[3]] **To pay** is what the customer pays.

![The bill. The Offer code box is marked 1, the Festive 10% off silk line is marked 2 and To pay 13,210 is marked 3.](8-bill.webp "The line Taken off lines by hand adds up the discounts you gave in step 7.")

## 9. Choose how they pay

1. [[1]] Click **Cash**, **UPI**, **Card** or **Split**.
2. [[2]] For cash, type the money the customer gave you. Leave it empty if they gave the exact amount. **Give back** shows the change.

![The Payment box. The Cash, UPI, Card and Split buttons are marked 1 and the Cash received box, with 14500 typed, is marked 2.](9-payment.webp "Give back 1,290 is the change to hand back.")

- **UPI**: you can type the UPI reference (UTR). It is optional.
- **Card**: you can type the last 4 digits or the approval code. Never type the full card number.
- **Split**: part UPI, part cash, and so on. Type the amount for each part. **Fill the rest** puts the remaining amount in the empty row. **Add part** adds another row.

![Split payment with a UPI row and a Cash row, and the Add part and Fill the rest buttons.](9-split.webp "The parts must add up to the bill exactly.")

## 10. Press Complete sale

[[1]] Click **Complete sale**. The button shows the amount to pay.

![The Complete sale 13,210 button is marked 1.](10-complete-sale.webp "If the button is grey, the line under it says what is missing.")

The stock comes off straight away.

## 11. Give the change and print the receipt

1. [[1]] **Give back** shows the change to hand over (only for cash).
2. [[2]] Click **Print receipt**.
3. [[3]] Click **New sale** for the next customer.

![Sale complete SO-000004. Give back 1,290 is marked 1, Print receipt is marked 2 and New sale is marked 3.](11-sale-complete.webp "View order opens the full order.")

More about receipts: [Print or reprint a receipt](/help/orders/receipts).

## On a phone

New sale does not open on a phone. You see *Sell from the counter computer or a tablet*. Use the shop's computer or a tablet.

![ScaleEzy on a phone showing Sell from the counter computer or a tablet, with a Back to orders button.](12-on-a-phone-phone.webp "Everything else in Orders works on a phone.")

## Common problems

:::faq I don't see New sale
Your role cannot sell at the counter, or you are on a phone. Sales staff, admins and owners can sell. Ask your shop owner if you need it.
:::

:::faq Complete sale stays grey
Read the line under the button. It tells you what is missing, for example *Add the customer's name.*, *Enter the customer's phone number.* or *Say why money is coming off the bill.*
:::

:::faq The item says None here or Not sold here
This store has none of it left, or the item is not sold at this store. Check the store name at the top of the screen. Check [Where is it?](/help/shelves/where-is-it) to see if another store has it.
:::

:::faq "Prices changed. Check the bill, then press Complete sale again."
A price or offer changed while you were billing. Check the new **To pay** and press **Complete sale** again.
:::

:::faq I don't see the % button
Your role cannot give discounts by hand. Ask a manager or the owner.
:::
