The **Products** page lists everything you sell. With many products, the search box and the filters find the ones you want in a few seconds.

## 1. Search, filters and the count

![The top of the Products page: 1 the search box, 2 the filters, 3 how many products match, 4 Sort.](1-search-and-filters.webp "These stay at the top of the screen while you scroll down the list.")

[[1]] Type in the search box: a product's name, its code, a barcode or SKU, or a colour. The list changes as you type. When you type or scan a whole code or barcode, that product comes first.

[[2]] The filters: **Type**, **Colour**, **Price**, **Fabric**, **Craft**, **Brand**, **Stock** and **Photos**. A filter that has only one choice for your shop is not shown.

[[3]] How many products match, out of all of them, and how many colours they have between them.

[[4]] **Sort** changes the order: newest or oldest first, price low to high or high to low, most or least in stock, or name A to Z.

## 2. Pick a colour

![The Colour filter open: a dot, a name and a count for each colour. Blue is marked 1.](2-colour-menu.webp "Navy, indigo and peacock all count as Blue.")

Click **Colour**. Each colour has its dot and, beside it, how many products you would see if you picked it. Similar shades are grouped, so Navy, Indigo and Peacock all come under [[1]] **Blue**. Tick more than one to see either.

The numbers in every filter count only what your other filters leave, so a choice never leads to an empty list by surprise.

## 3. What you see after picking

![Products with Colour: Blue chosen. The chip is marked 1, Clear all 2 and the count 3.](3-blue-only.webp "In the Photos view, each card shows the colour you picked.")

[[1]] Each choice you make shows as a chip. Click its **✕** to take that one off.

[[2]] **Clear all** takes every filter and the search off at once.

[[3]] The count says how many products are left.

In the **Photos** view, each product shows only the colours that match, so picking Blue shows the blue sarees, not whichever colour comes first.

## 4. Price

![The Price filter open: ready-made price ranges, the first marked 1, and From and To boxes marked 2.](4-price.webp)

[[1]] Pick a ready-made range: under ₹1,000, ₹1,000 to ₹5,000, ₹5,000 to ₹15,000, or above ₹15,000. Each shows how many products it would leave.

[[2]] Or type your own **From** and **To**, then press Enter.

A product's price is the price of each colour, or the product's price when a colour has none of its own.

## 5. Sort

![The Sort menu open, marked 1.](5-sort.webp)

[[1]] Choose how the list is ordered. The search and filters stay as they are.

## 6. More to know

- **Stock** finds what is in stock, running low or sold out. **Photos** finds products that still have no photos.
- A filter looks at each colour on its own. "Blue and above ₹15,000" finds a product only if its blue one costs more than ₹15,000.
- The list shows 50 products at a time. Press **Show 50 more** at the bottom for the next ones.
- The search and filters are in the page address. **Back** after opening a product brings you to the same list, and you can send the page's link to someone on your team.
- The list above the filters (**Active & Drafts**, **Drafts only**, **Published only**, **Archived**, **Trash**) still chooses which products are searched.

## Common problems

:::faq Nothing matches all of these
Your filters together leave nothing. Take one off with its ✕, or press **Clear all filters**.
:::

:::faq A colour I expected is under another name
Colours are grouped into families: Maroon and Crimson are Red, Mustard is Yellow, Off White is White. A colour no family knows keeps its own name in the list.
:::

:::faq A product's photo is in another colour
A colour with no photo of its own shows the product's main photo. The colour's name on the card is the one that matched. Add a photo for that colour on the product's **Photos** tab.
:::
