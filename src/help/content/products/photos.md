A product can have general photos (the fabric, the border, the drape) and its own photos for each size and colour. Customers who choose a colour see the photo of that colour.

## 1. Open the Images tab

1. Open the product from **Products**.
2. Click the [[1]] **Images** tab. The red button [[2]] shows how many sizes and colours have no photo yet. Clicking it also opens the Images tab.

![The product page for Chanderi Dupatta. The Images tab is marked 1 and the red 1 variant has no image button is marked 2.](1-open.webp "The red button goes away when every colour has a photo.")

## 2. Add photos

The tab has one part for each size and colour, here **Peach · Free**.

1. Click [[1]] **Add photos** in that part.
2. Choose the photos on your computer or phone. **Several at once is fine.** They upload straight away.

![The Images tab. Add photos for Peach · Free is marked 1. Under it: No photo of Peach · Free yet.](2-images-tab.webp "No photo of Peach yet: a customer choosing it would see another colour.")

:::note Photo size
A photo can be up to 5 MB. Most phone photos are smaller than that.
:::

## 3. Choose the main photo, or remove one

The first photo in each part becomes the main photo.

- [[1]] **PRIMARY** marks the main photo.
- [[2]] Click the star on another photo to make it the main photo (*Use this as the main photo*).
- [[3]] Click the red **✕** to delete a photo. ScaleEzy asks *Delete this photo?*. Press **Delete** to remove it.

![Two general photos and one Peach photo. PRIMARY is marked 1, the star on the second photo 2 and the red cross 3.](3-main-photo.webp "Every size and colour has at least one photo.")

:::warning Deleting a photo cannot be undone
The photo is removed from the product and from storage. To get it back you must upload it again.
:::

## Make model photos (the four catalog views)

ScaleEzy can turn your own photo of a garment into four catalogue pictures of a model wearing it: **Front**, **Sitting**, **Right** and **Back**. Your own photo stays as it is.

It works for sarees, lehengas, anarkalis, shararas and kurtis (or kurtas).

**When you add a product:** on the photo step, tick **Make the four catalog views for the Red one** (the colour's name). They are made after you publish. You do not have to wait. See [Add a product](/help/products/add-a-product).

**For a product you already have:** on its **Images** tab, in **Make the four catalog views**:

1. Choose **Which colour to photograph**.
2. Press **MAKE THE FOUR VIEWS**.

You can carry on with other work. The card says how far it is, for example *Making the Red views… 1 of 4 done*, and ScaleEzy tells you when they are ready. When one colour is done, the other colours can be made from it.

Not happy with a set? Under **Not happy with a set?**, choose **Which set to make again**. It uses one generation from your allowance.

### When the photos are ready

A notice appears at the bottom left of every screen, for example *The Red photographs are ready* or *The Red photographs did not finish*. Press it to open that product's **Images** tab. Press **×** to close it.

If you are signed out when they finish, the notice waits for you. Closing it closes it for everyone in your shop, so tell the person who asked for the photos. The result stays on the product's **Images** tab either way.

### Waiting in line, and stopping

Your shop makes one set at a time, and each takes about a minute. Other sets wait their turn, and the card says, for example, *The Red views are next in line…*. Up to 10 sets can wait at once.

Press **Stop** to stop a set. Views already made are kept. A set you stop is not counted against your allowance.

:::note Who can use it, and how many
It uses the **4-View Catalog Try-On** service, which ScaleEzy switches on for your shop. Only people with the permission **Generate try-on images** can use it: the owner, an admin or an inventory manager. Each run that finishes, or fails, counts against your shop's monthly allowance. A run you stop is not counted. See [APIs and services](/help/settings/apis-and-services).
:::

## Common problems

:::faq "File is too large. Maximum size is 5MB."
Make the photo smaller first, for example with the crop or resize tool of your phone's photo app. Then add it again.
:::

:::faq The red "variants have no image" button does not go away
Each size and colour needs its own photo, not only the product as a whole. Look for parts that say *no photos* and add one there.
:::

:::faq I cannot add photos
Adding and deleting photos needs permission to change products. The owner, an admin or an inventory manager can do it.
:::

:::faq "3 of 4 views were made"
The photo studio stopped part-way. The views it made are kept. To make the rest, choose that colour again and press **MAKE THE FOUR VIEWS**. It uses one generation.
:::

:::faq "The photo studio could not be reached just now" or "stopped responding"
The service that makes the photos had a problem. Your photos are safe. Try again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, contact ScaleEzy support. Tell them the product and roughly when it happened. ScaleEzy sees the exact reason for every set that fails.
:::

:::faq "This workspace has used its …"
Your shop's allowance for this month is used up. Ask ScaleEzy to raise it. See [APIs and services](/help/settings/apis-and-services).
:::

:::faq "You already have … sets of photographs being made"
Up to 10 sets can wait at once. Let some finish, then start the rest.
:::

:::faq It says "next in line" for a long time
Another set from your shop is being made first. Each takes about a minute. If nothing changes for 10 minutes, contact ScaleEzy support.
:::

:::faq "We can only photograph sarees, kurtis, anarkalis, lehengas and shararas on a model"
Model photos work only for those garments. If the product is one of them, set its dress type to match. Otherwise, upload your own photos.
:::
