A product is always in one of four states:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **DRAFT** | Not on sale yet. Imported products start as drafts. |
| **ACTIVE** | Published and on sale. |
| **ARCHIVED** | Put away. Hidden from the normal list, but kept with all its history. |
| **TRASHED** | In the Trash. Not offered when you sell, buy or move stock. |

The Products list shows **Active & Drafts**. To see the others, use the list box at the top of **Products**: **Drafts only**, **Published only**, **Archived** or **Trash**.

## 1. Publish one product

1. Open the product. Its state shows next to the name, here [[1]] **DRAFT**.
2. Click [[2]] **Publish**.

![A draft product, Organza Party Saree. DRAFT is marked 1 and the Publish button is marked 2.](1-publish-one.webp "Archive and Move to Trash are next to Publish.")

If the product has no photos, or no sizes and colours, ScaleEzy asks first. You can press **Cancel** and add what is missing, or press [[1]] **Publish anyway**.

![The question Publish Organza Party Saree? with the message This product has no photographs at all. Publish anyway is marked 1.](2-publish-anyway.webp "A product with a photo is published straight away, without this question.")

## 2. Take a product off sale

The product is now [[1]] **ACTIVE**. To take it off sale but keep it, click [[2]] **Unpublish**. It goes back to a draft. Its stock and history do not change.

![The product is ACTIVE, marked 1. The Unpublish button is marked 2.](3-unpublish.webp)

## 3. Publish many products at once

1. On **Products**, choose **Drafts only** at the top.
2. Tick the box in front of each product, here [[1]] and [[2]].
3. A bar shows how many are selected. Click [[3]] **Publish** with the number, for example **Publish 2**.

![The Drafts only list with two products ticked, marked 1 and 2. Publish 2 is marked 3.](4-publish-many.webp "Clear removes all the ticks.")

4. ScaleEzy asks *Publish 2 products?* and says if some have no photo. Press [[1]] **Publish 2** (or **Publish 2 anyway**).

![The question Publish 2 products? Publish 2 anyway is marked 1.](5-publish-many-confirm.webp "They go on sale immediately.")

To take many products off sale, tick published products and press **Unpublish** with the number.

## 4. Archive a product

Archive products you no longer sell but want to keep, for example last season's designs.

Open the product and click [[1]] **Archive**. The other choice is [[2]] **Move to Trash**, see step 5.

![The product buttons. Archive is marked 1 and Move to Trash is marked 2.](6-archive-or-trash.webp)

The product is now [[1]] **ARCHIVED**. To bring it back, click [[2]] **Restore**.

![The product shows ARCHIVED, marked 1. The Restore button is marked 2.](7-archived.webp "Find archived products with Archived at the top of Products.")

## 5. Move a product to the Trash

Use the Trash for products added by mistake.

1. Open the product and click **Move to Trash**.
2. ScaleEzy tells you what happens. Press [[1]] **Move to Trash**.

![The question Move to Trash. The red Move to Trash button is marked 1.](8-move-to-trash.webp "If the product still has stock, the message says so. The stock stays where it is.")

A product in the Trash is not offered when you take stock in or out, raise a purchase order or sell. To see it, choose [[1]] **Trash** at the top of **Products**. [[2]] Your product is in the list.

![The Products list filtered to Trash, marked 1. Organza Party Saree with status TRASHED is marked 2.](10-trash-list.webp)

To take it out of the Trash, open it and click **Restore**.

## 6. Delete a product for ever

![A trashed product. Restore is marked 1. Permanently Delete is grey, with the reason under it, marked 2.](9-in-the-trash.webp "Product must remain in Trash for 7 days before permanent deletion.")

[[1]] **Restore** brings the product back. [[2]] **Permanently Delete** erases it. It only works when all of these are true:

- The product has been in the Trash for **7 days**.
- It has **no stock**.
- It never had any stock movement, was never in a stock count and is on no purchase order.

Until then the button is grey and the reason is written under it. Most products that were ever sold can never be deleted for ever. Keep them archived instead.

When deleting is possible, ScaleEzy asks you to type **CONFIRM** in capital letters, then press **Delete Permanently**.

:::warning Delete forever cannot be undone
The product, all its sizes and colours and all its photos are erased. There is no copy anywhere. If you only want the product out of the way, leave it in the Trash or archive it.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq Archive or Move to Trash does nothing, or shows a permission message
Archive, Restore, Move to Trash and Permanently Delete are for the owner or an admin only. Inventory managers can publish and unpublish, but not archive or delete.
:::

:::faq My product is not in the Products list
The list shows active products and drafts. Choose **Archived** or **Trash** at the top to find the others.

The list also shows only the **50 newest** products. There is no search box and no next page on **Products**. To find an older product:

- On a computer, type its name, product code or SKU in the search box at the top of the screen and press **Enter**. ScaleEzy opens it.
- Or open **Inventory** and search there. See [See your stock](/help/inventory/see-your-stock).

**Export** on **Products** also saves only the products in the list, so at most 50.
:::

:::faq I cannot find a trashed product when I sell
That is on purpose. Restore it first, then sell it.
:::
