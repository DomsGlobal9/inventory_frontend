A published product is sold at the till, and **online** too: your online shop, reel links, other websites linked to ScaleEzy, and Shopify.

Choose **Till only** for a product you want to sell only at the counter, for example a one-off bridal piece or something too delicate to post. It leaves every online place at once, and still sells at the till.

| | Till | Online shop, reel links, websites | Shopify |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Till and online** | Sold | Shown and sold | Kept in step |
| **Till only** | Sold | Hidden | Archived (a product made by hand in Shopify shows 0 in stock) |

Every product starts as **Till and online**. Drafts and materials are not on sale anywhere, so they have no choice yet.

## 1. One product

1. Open the product.
2. In [[1]] **Where it is sold**, choose **Till and online** or **Till only**. It saves at once.

![The product page. The Where it is sold box with Till and online and Till only is marked 1.](1-product.webp "The Shopify box under it says when a product is till only.")

## 2. Many products at once

1. On **Products**, tick the products. The [[1]] **Online** column shows **On** or **Till only** for each.
2. In the bar, click [[2]] **Stop selling online** with the number, or **Sell online** for products that are till only.

![The Products list with products ticked. The Online column is marked 1 and the Stop selling online button is marked 2.](2-many.webp "Only published products count; drafts are left out.")

3. For **Stop selling online**, ScaleEzy asks once. Click [[1]] **Stop selling online** to confirm.

![The question Stop selling 2 products online? The Stop selling online button is marked 1.](3-confirm.webp)

A product that cannot go online is skipped, and the message says which one and why: a product with **no photo** (add one first) or one that is **not published**.

## 3. See which products are till only

On **Products**, open the [[1]] **Sold online** filter and choose **Till only**. **Sold online** shows the rest, and **Not on sale** shows drafts and materials.

![The Sold online filter open, with Sold online, Till only and Not on sale. The filter is marked 1.](4-filter.webp)

## Good to know

- **A customer already has it in their bag.** When they check out, they are told it is no longer sold online and to take it out.
- **Reel links** that hold it hide it until you sell it online again. You cannot add a till-only product to a reel link.
- **Shopify**: switching back to **Till and online** puts it back on Shopify by itself.
- The **Export** of Products has a **SellOnline** column: *Yes* or *Till only*.
