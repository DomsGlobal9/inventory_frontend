**Try-On** lets a customer see themselves wearing a garment before they buy it. Every product has its own **Try-On QR code**. You can put it on the garment's tag or next to it in the shop. The customer scans it with their phone camera.

## 1. Find the QR code

1. Open the product from **Products**. It opens on the [[1]] **Overview** tab.
2. On the right, under **Identifiers**, is the [[2]] **Try-On QR Code**.

![The product page for Kanchipuram Silk Saree on the Overview tab, marked 1. The Try-On QR Code box on the right is marked 2.](1-open.webp "The product code is shown just above the QR code.")

## 2. Use it

[[1]] This is the product's code. Under a published product the line says *A customer scans this and sees themselves wearing it.*

![The Identifiers box with the product code and the QR code, marked 1.](2-the-code.webp "The code is made from your shop and the product code, so it does not change when you edit the product.")

## 3. Print a tag for each colour

A tag hangs on one piece, so each colour gets **its own** code. A customer who scans the tag on the red saree sees the red one on themselves.

1. Under the QR code, press **Print tags for each colour**.
2. [[1]] Every colour and size is ticked. Untick the ones you don't need.
3. Each tag shows your shop's logo and name, *See it on yourself*, the code, the product name, the colour, the price and the variant code.
4. Press [[2]] **Print N tags**. Your printer's window opens. Only the tags are printed.

![Try-on tags for Kanchipuram Silk Saree. The colour tick boxes are marked 1 and Print tags is marked 2.](4-tags.webp "The logo comes from Settings → Name, logo and bill details.")

If the window says *This product has no colours or sizes yet*, add them on the **Variants** tab first. See [Variants and barcodes](/help/products/variants-and-barcodes).

## 4. It works only for published products

A draft product also shows a QR code, with the line [[1]] *This works once the product is published — a scan finds nothing while it is a draft.*

![The Identifiers box of a draft product. The line This works once the product is published is marked 1.](3-draft.webp "Publish the product first, then the scan works.")

For the scan to work:

- The product must be **published** (status ACTIVE). See [Publish, archive and delete](/help/products/publish-archive-delete).
- The product must have **at least one photo**. The customer is shown this photo, so use a clear photo of the whole garment. See [Product photos](/help/products/photos).

:::note Try-On is switched on by ScaleEzy
If the box says *Try-On is not switched on for this workspace yet, so there is no code to print.*, Try-On is not active for your shop yet. Contact ScaleEzy support to ask for it. See [Get support](/help/settings/get-support).
:::

## Show it on a customer at the counter

The QR code is for a customer browsing alone. When the customer is at your counter, you can make the picture yourself, from a photo you take of them.

1. On the product's **Overview** tab, press **See it on the customer**. It is just above the QR code.
2. [[1]] **Which one are they holding?** Press the colour in the customer's hands. The picture is made from that colour's photo.
3. Press **Take a photograph**. On a phone or tablet, the camera opens. A photo you already took? Press **Choose a photo**.
4. Press **See it on them**. The picture is ready in a few seconds. **Open it** shows it bigger. **Another photo** starts again.

![See it on the customer, for Kanchipuram Silk Saree. The colours, marked 1: Maroon with its own photo, and Red marked main photo, because it has no photo of its own yet.](5-counter.webp "A product with one colour skips this question.")

- The photo of the customer is used **once** to make the picture, then deleted. Nothing is kept.
- A colour marked *main photo* has no photo of its own yet. Its picture would be made from the product's main photo, which may be another colour. Add that colour's photo on the **Images** tab first. See [Product photos](/help/products/photos).
- The button is greyed out while the product has no photo at all.

## Common problems

:::faq The customer scans the code and sees nothing
Check that the product is published and has a photo. A draft, an archived product or a product with no photo shows nothing.
:::

:::faq There is no QR code on the product page
Try-On is not switched on for your shop. The box then says so in words. Contact ScaleEzy support.
:::

:::faq See it on the customer is greyed out
The product has no photo yet. Add one on the **Images** tab.
:::
