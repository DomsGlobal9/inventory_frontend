**Try-On** lets a customer see themselves wearing a garment before they buy it. Every product has its own **Try-On QR code**. You can put it on the garment's tag or next to it in the shop. The customer scans it with their phone camera.

## 1. Find the QR code

1. Open the product from **Products**. It opens on the [[1]] **Overview** tab.
2. On the right, under **Identifiers**, is the [[2]] **Try-On QR Code**.

![The product page for Kanchipuram Silk Saree on the Overview tab, marked 1. The Try-On QR Code box on the right is marked 2.](1-open.webp "The product code is shown just above the QR code.")

## 2. Use it

[[1]] This is the code. Under a published product the line says *A customer scans this and sees themselves wearing it.* To print the code, take a screenshot of it or show this screen to your designer.

![The Identifiers box with the product code and the QR code, marked 1.](2-the-code.webp "The code is made from your shop and the product code, so it does not change when you edit the product.")

## 3. It works only for published products

A draft product also shows a QR code, with the line [[1]] *This works once the product is published — a scan finds nothing while it is a draft.*

![The Identifiers box of a draft product. The line This works once the product is published is marked 1.](3-draft.webp "Publish the product first, then the scan works.")

For the scan to work:

- The product must be **published** (status ACTIVE). See [Publish, archive and delete](/help/products/publish-archive-delete).
- The product must have **at least one photo**. The customer is shown this photo, so use a clear photo of the whole garment. See [Product photos](/help/products/photos).

:::note Try-On is switched on by ScaleEzy
If the box says *Try-On is not switched on for this workspace yet, so there is no code to print.*, Try-On is not active for your shop yet. Contact ScaleEzy support to ask for it. See [Get support](/help/settings/get-support).
:::

## Common problems

:::faq The customer scans the code and sees nothing
Check that the product is published and has a photo. A draft, an archived product or a product with no photo shows nothing.
:::

:::faq There is no QR code on the product page
Try-On is not switched on for your shop. The box then says so in words. Contact ScaleEzy support.
:::
