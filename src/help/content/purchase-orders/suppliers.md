## 1. Open Suppliers

1. In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Purchase Orders**.
2. At the top, click the [[2]] **Suppliers** tab.

![The Purchase Orders screen. Purchase Orders is marked 1 in the menu and the Suppliers tab is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Only people allowed to see suppliers have this tab.")

## 2. The supplier list

Each card shows the supplier's code, email, phone, how many purchase orders you have raised with them (**Total POs**) and whether they are **Active**.

- [[1]] **Add Supplier** adds a new one.
- [[2]] Click a card to open that supplier.

![The Suppliers list. Add Supplier is marked 1 and the Kanchi Weavers card is marked 2.](2-list.webp "Search by name or supplier code.")

## 3. Add a supplier

Click **Add Supplier** and fill in:

1. [[1]] **Company Name**. This is the only box you must fill.
2. [[2]] **Email**. Needed to email purchase orders from ScaleEzy.
3. [[3]] **Phone**. Needed to send orders on WhatsApp. A 10-digit number is taken as an Indian number; for other countries type the + and country code.
4. Add the **Address** if you like, then press [[4]] **Create Supplier**.

![The Add New Supplier form filled in for Surat Silk Mills. Company Name is marked 1, Email 2, Phone 3 and Create Supplier 4.](3-add-supplier.webp "ScaleEzy gives the supplier its own code.")

## 4. The supplier page

Open a supplier to see everything about them.

- [[1]] **Edit Supplier**: change the details. Untick **Active Supplier** to stop ordering from them, without losing their history.
- [[2]] **Procurement Overview**: **Total Orders**, **Open Orders** (drafts and orders not fully received yet), **Total Spend** (the value of every order raised with them) and **Last Order Date**.

![The Kanchi Weavers page. Edit Supplier is marked 1 and Procurement Overview is marked 2.](4-supplier-page.webp "Contact Details are on the left.")

## 5. Items We Buy Here

This list fills itself: every item you put on a purchase order to this supplier is added, with **Their Price** (taken from the first order you raised with them) and how many you have **In Stock**.

1. [[1]] **PREFERRED** means this is the supplier you usually buy this item from. [Reorder suggestions](/help/purchase-orders/reorder) order it from them.
2. [[2]] Start a new purchase order to this supplier for this item.
3. [[3]] The star makes this supplier the preferred one for the item.
4. [[4]] The red bin removes the item from this supplier's list. The item itself is not deleted.

![Items We Buy Here for Kanchi Weavers. The PREFERRED label on the red saree is marked 1. On the maroon saree, the order button is 2, the star 3 and the bin 4.](5-items-we-buy-here.webp "In Stock counts all your stores together.")

:::tip See it from the item's side
To compare every supplier of one colour and size, with their prices side by side and the cheapest in green, open the product and click the truck button on its **Variants** tab. See [Who supplies this item](/help/products/product-page#who-supplies-this-item).
:::

## 6. Recent Purchase Orders

At the bottom are this supplier's orders with their status and amount. [[1]] Click one to open it.

![Recent Purchase Orders for Kanchi Weavers. The first order is marked 1.](6-recent-orders.webp "SENT means waiting for delivery. RECEIVED means everything arrived.")

:::note New PO here opens the purchase order list
To order from this supplier straight away, use the order button next to an item in **Items We Buy Here**, or see [Raise and send a purchase order](/help/purchase-orders/create-a-purchase-order).
:::

## Who can do this

Owners, admins and managers can add and change suppliers. Stock room staff and salespeople cannot see suppliers.

## Common problems

:::faq Send on WhatsApp is greyed out on an order
The supplier has no phone number, or it is not a mobile number WhatsApp can use. Open **Edit Supplier** and type the 10-digit mobile number. Also check the order has a store under **Deliver to**.
:::

:::faq Email to supplier is greyed out on an order
The supplier has no email address. Add one with **Edit Supplier**. Also check the order has a store under **Deliver to**.
:::

:::faq Items We Buy Here is empty
Nothing has been ordered from this supplier in ScaleEzy yet. Items appear after your first purchase order to them.
:::

:::faq "... is already a supplier"
Another supplier already has that name, even if the capitals or spaces differ. Use that supplier, or add something to the name to tell them apart, such as the town. A supplier that was switched off still counts: switch it back on instead.
:::
