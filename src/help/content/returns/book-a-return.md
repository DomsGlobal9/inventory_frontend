Booking a return only records that pieces are coming back. **Nothing goes back into stock yet.** That happens when the return is checked. See [Receive, check and finish a return](/help/returns/finish-a-return).

:::note Who can book a return
You need to see customers and to log returns. With the standard roles, that is **admins and the owner**. Sales staff cannot log returns, and stock room staff cannot open Customers. Your owner can change this in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
:::

## 1. Open the customer's Dispatches

1. In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Customers** and open the customer. See [Customers](/help/customers/customers).
2. Click the [[2]] **Dispatches** tab. You see everything that was sent or handed to them, with the order number.
3. On the correct row, click [[3]] **Return**.

![Lakshmi Narayanan's page. Customers is marked 1 in the menu, the Dispatches tab 2 and the Return button on the SO-000001 row 3.](1-open.webp "A counter sale shows here too, because the customer took the pieces with them.")

If a row says **All returned**, every piece from it has already come back.

## 2. Say what is coming back

1. [[1]] **All of it** fills in every piece that can still come back.
2. [[2]] Or use **−** and **+** on each item.

![The Book in a return box. All of it is marked 1 and the Kanchipuram Silk Saree line, set to 1, is marked 2. The Chanderi Dupatta line is grey.](2-what-is-coming-back.webp "Nothing left to return means those pieces are already on another open return.")

The line at the top counts the pieces, for example *1 piece from DSP-000001 will be booked in as coming back.*

## 3. Say why

1. [[1]] Choose why it is coming back: *Damaged on the way to the customer*, *Faulty or badly made*, *We sent the wrong thing*, *Size did not fit*, *Customer changed their mind* or *Something else*.
2. [[2]] Add a note if it helps, for example what is wrong with it. This is optional.

![The reason list set to Faulty or badly made is marked 1. The note box with Zari coming loose near the pallu is marked 2.](3-why.webp "The reason is needed. It shows you later why things come back.")

## 4. Book it in

Click [[1]] **Book it in**.

![The Book it in button at the bottom right of the box is marked 1.](4-book-it-in.webp "You see Return raised when it is saved.")

## 5. Find it in Returns

The return is now in **Returns** as [[1]] **Requested**. When the goods arrive, the stock room receives and checks it.

![The Customer Returns list. The new return for SO-000001, Requested, reason Faulty or badly made, is marked 1.](5-on-returns-list.webp "The list shows the reason in the same words as the return form.")

Next: [Receive, check and finish a return](/help/returns/finish-a-return).

## Common problems

:::faq I don't see the Return button
Your role cannot log returns. Or every piece has already come back and the row says **All returned**.
:::

:::faq "Choose how many of each item are coming back."
All the numbers are 0. Click **All of it** or use **+** on the item.
:::

:::faq "Say why it is coming back"
Choose a reason in **Why is it coming back?** before you click **Book it in**.
:::

:::faq I booked a return by mistake
Open it in **Returns** and click **Turn down**. Nothing goes back into stock, and the pieces can be returned properly later.
:::
