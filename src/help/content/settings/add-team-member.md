Give every person their own login. Then each person sees only what their job needs, and the app records who did what.

Only the owner and people who can **Manage the team and their roles** see **Team & Users**. In the ready roles, that is the owner and **ADMIN**.

## 1. Open Team & Users

1. Open **Settings** and click [[1]] **Team & Users**.
2. Click [[2]] **Add Team Member**.

![The Team and Users screen with the team list. Team and Users is marked 1 and Add Team Member is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Everyone who can sign in to your shop is listed here.")

## 2. Fill in the form

1. [[1]] **Name**: the person's full name.
2. [[2]] **Email**: the email they will sign in with. Each person needs a different email.
3. [[3]] **Role**: what they can see and do. See the table below.
4. [[4]] **Auto-generate** makes a strong password for you. To type your own, click **Custom** and type at least 6 characters.
5. Press [[5]] **Create Account**.

![The Add Team Member form for Suresh Babu. Name is marked 1, Email 2, Role 3, Auto-generate 4 and Create Account 5.](2-fill-in.webp "Auto-generate is chosen when the form opens.")

## 3. Choose the right role

![The Role list open, showing ADMIN, INVENTORY MANAGER, SALES, SUPER ADMIN and WAREHOUSE.](3-roles.webp "Your own roles from Roles and Permissions also appear in this list.")

| Role | In one line |
|---|---|
| **SUPER ADMIN** | The owner. Can do everything. Only the owner can give this role. |
| **ADMIN** | Almost everything, including the team, costs and money reports. |
| **INVENTORY MANAGER** | Buys and manages stock: products, suppliers, purchase orders, counts, shelves, stores and costs. Does not sell or manage the team. |
| **SALES** | Sells at the counter, takes orders and adds customers. Sees products and where they are kept. Does not see costs. |
| **WAREHOUSE** | Receives, moves and counts stock, sends out orders and handles returns. Does not see money or customers. |

Need something in between? Make your own role. See [Roles and permissions](/help/settings/roles).

:::note You cannot give more than you have
An admin cannot give someone a role that can do more than the admin can. Only the owner can make someone **SUPER ADMIN**.
:::

## 4. Share the login

After **Create Account**, a box shows the new login.

1. [[1]] The email and password. Keep this box open until the person has the login.
2. If email works for your shop, ScaleEzy **has already emailed the login** to them. The box then says **Sent to** and their email. [[2]] **Send again** sends the same email once more.
3. [[3]] **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the login message ready. You choose the contact and send it.
4. [[4]] **Copy** copies the email and password, so you can paste them anywhere.

![The Credentials for Suresh Babu box. The email and password are marked 1, Send again 2, WhatsApp 3 and Copy 4.](4-share-login.webp "The password stays the same until you change it.")

If the email could not be sent, the box says **Not emailed** with the reason, and the button says **Send by email**. Use **WhatsApp** or **Copy** instead.

The new person can now sign in. See [Sign in](/help/start/sign-in).

:::tip Forgot to note the password?
You can see it again later. See [Change a role, reset a password, switch someone off](/help/settings/manage-team).
:::

## Common problems

:::faq "A team member with this email already exists"
Someone in your shop already uses this email. Use a different email, or find that person in the list.
:::

:::faq I don't see Team & Users
Only the owner and people who can manage the team see it. Ask your shop owner.
:::

:::faq The person never got the email
Ask them to check their spam folder. Or open their **Password** in the team list and share the login by **WhatsApp** or **Copy**.
:::

:::faq Create Account does nothing with a Custom password
A custom password needs at least 6 characters.
:::
