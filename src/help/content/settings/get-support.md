Something not working, or a question this guide does not answer? Raise a **ticket**. The ScaleEzy support team reads it and replies inside ScaleEzy.

## 1. Open Help & Support

1. In the menu, click [[1]] **Settings**. On a phone, open the menu with **☰** first.
2. Click [[2]] **Help & Support**. Everyone has it, whatever their role.
3. Click [[3]] **New Ticket**.

![Settings for a salesperson. Settings is marked 1 in the left menu, Help and Support 2 and New Ticket 3.](1-open.webp "Your tickets are listed here. The list is empty until you raise one.")

## 2. Fill in the ticket

1. [[1]] **Subject**: a short summary, for example *How do I print labels for a new rack?*
2. [[2]] **Category**: what kind of problem it is. See the list below.
3. [[3]] **Priority**: **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Urgent**. Use **Urgent** only when you cannot sell or work at all.
4. [[4]] **Describe the issue**: what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Say which store you were in.
5. Press [[5]] **Submit Ticket**.

![The new ticket form. Subject is marked 1, Category 2, Priority 3, Describe the issue 4 and Submit Ticket 5.](2-new-ticket.webp "Cancel goes back to your list without sending anything.")

![The Category list open: Something is broken, How do I..., Billing, Feature request and Other.](3-categories.webp "How do I... is chosen when the form opens.")

:::tip Help us help you faster
Write the order number, SKU or product name if the problem is about one of them. Write the exact words of any error message.
:::

## 3. Read the reply

1. Open **Settings → Help & Support**. Your ticket is in the list with its status: **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed**.
2. Click the ticket to open the conversation. Replies from ScaleEzy show **Scaleezy Support** next to the name.
3. To answer, type in **Type a reply...** at the bottom and press the send button.

If you reply to a **Resolved** ticket, it opens again. A **Closed** ticket has no reply box. Raise a new ticket if you need more help.

:::note Who sees which tickets
Staff see only the tickets they raised. The owner and people who can manage the team see every ticket from your shop.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq "Please enter the subject"
**Subject** and **Describe the issue** must both be filled in before **Submit Ticket** works.
:::

:::faq I cannot find a ticket my colleague raised
Staff only see their own tickets. Ask your owner, who sees every ticket from the shop.
:::

:::faq I cannot sign in, so I cannot raise a ticket
Ask your shop owner or admin to check your login. See [Sign in](/help/start/sign-in).
:::
