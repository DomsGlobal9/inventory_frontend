Everything about one person is on their card in **Settings → Team & Users**.

## 1. Find the person

Open **Settings → Team & Users**. Each person has a card with:

1. [[1]] their **role**,
2. [[2]] **ACTIVE** or **INACTIVE**,
3. [[3]] **Password**.

![Team and Users with Kiran Rao's card. The role box is marked 1, ACTIVE 2 and the Password button 3.](1-a-person.webp "Under the status is the day they were last active, or Never.")

Your own card is greyed out. Nobody can change their own role, status or password here. An admin also cannot change the owner's card.

## 2. Change a role

Click the role box and choose the new role. It saves at once. There is no Save button.

![The role list open on Anjali Devi's card, showing ADMIN, INVENTORY MANAGER, SALES, SUPER ADMIN and WAREHOUSE.](2-change-role.webp "The tick shows the role the person has now.")

The change works straight away. They do not need to sign in again, but their menu changes only the next time they open or refresh ScaleEzy. To see what each role can do, read [Roles and permissions](/help/settings/roles).

## 3. See the current password

A team member forgot their password? You can see it, or set a new one.

1. Click **Password** on their card.
2. [[1]] **View Current** is open.
3. Press [[2]] **Reveal Password**. The password shows, with **Email**, **WhatsApp** and **Copy** buttons to share it again.
4. To give a new password instead, click [[3]] **Set New**. See the next step.

![Kiran Rao's Password box. View Current is marked 1, Reveal Password 2 and Set New 3.](3-password.webp "Every time a password is revealed, it is recorded.")

To reveal a password you also need the permission **Read a team member's password**. The owner and **ADMIN** have it.

If it says *No viewable password on file for this account*, use **Set New** instead.

## 4. Set a new password

Use this when a password may be known by someone else.

1. In the **Password** box, click **Set New**.
2. [[1]] Type a new password of at least 6 characters, and press [[2]] **Set This Password**.
3. Or press [[3]] **Or Auto-generate Instead** to let ScaleEzy make one.

![The Set New part of the Password box. The new password box is marked 1, Set This Password 2 and Or Auto-generate Instead 3.](4-set-new.webp "The new password shows in the box after you press either button.")

:::warning A new password signs the person out everywhere
As soon as you set a new password, the person is signed out on every phone and computer. They must sign in again with the new password. ScaleEzy also emails the new password to them when email works for your shop.
:::

## 5. Switch someone off

When a person leaves your shop, do not share their login with someone else. Switch them off.

Click [[1]] **ACTIVE** on their card. It changes to **INACTIVE** at once.

![Ravi Kumar's card with the status INACTIVE marked 1.](5-switched-off.webp "Click INACTIVE to switch the person back on.")

- The person is **signed out everywhere straight away** and cannot sign in.
- Their past sales, stock moves and history stay as they are.
- If you switch them back on, they must sign in again. Their old sign-ins do not come back.

:::note The last owner cannot be switched off
If only one person is **SUPER ADMIN**, ScaleEzy refuses to switch them off or change their role. Make someone else SUPER ADMIN first.
:::

## 6. See recent activity

Click **Recent Activity** at the top of **Team & Users**. It lists the last 30 things people did, newest first, with the date and time.

![The Recent Activity box listing actions such as Priya Reddy changed a team member's status and Priya Reddy created location, each with date and time.](6-recent-activity.webp "Click outside the box or the X to close it.")

## Common problems

:::faq The role box and Password are grey
It is your own card, or it is the owner's card and you are an admin. Ask the owner.
:::

:::faq "You cannot manage this account: it can do things you cannot"
The person's role can do more than yours. Only someone with at least the same permissions, or the owner, can change them.
:::

:::faq Reveal Password shows an error
Your role needs **Read a team member's password**. Ask the owner, or use **Set New** instead.
:::

:::faq The person says their login stopped working
Check their card. If it says **INACTIVE**, click it to switch them on. If someone set a new password, share the new one with them.
:::
