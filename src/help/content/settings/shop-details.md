Your shop details appear on the papers you give to other people: counter receipts, purchase orders and goods receipts. Only the owner can change them.

## 1. Open your shop's details

1. In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Settings**.
2. Under **Your shop**, click [[2]] **Name, logo and bill details**. Only the owner sees this link.

![The Settings page. Settings is marked 1 in the left menu and Name, logo and bill details, under Your shop, is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Your own profile is at the top of Settings. The shop's settings are in the cards under it.")

## 2. Shop name and logo

1. [[1]] Check the **Shop name**. Change it if needed.
2. Click [[2]] **Add logo** and choose a picture of your logo.

![The Shop details card. The Shop name box is marked 1 and the Add logo button is marked 2.](2-name-and-logo.webp "A square picture works best. PNG, JPG or SVG, up to 2MB.")

The logo is **saved as soon as you choose it**. You do not need to press Save for the logo.

After that, the button says **Change**, and a **Remove** button appears next to it.

:::tip Drag and drop
You can also drag the picture file from your computer and drop it on the logo box.
:::

## 3. Fill in the letterhead

Fill in only what you want printed. An empty box is simply left off the paper.

1. [[1]] **Address**: your full shop address with PIN code.
2. Also **Phone** and **Email**.
3. [[2]] **GSTIN**: your 15-character GST number. Small letters change to capitals by themselves.
4. [[3]] **Receipt footer**: one short line at the bottom of every counter receipt, for example your exchange rule. Up to 160 characters.

![The Letterhead part of the form. Address is marked 1, GSTIN 2 and Receipt footer 3.](3-letterhead.webp "Use short lines. A receipt roll is narrow.")

## 4. Say how your shop is registered for GST

Under **GST**, choose [[1]] what your shop is:

- **GST registered (Regular)**: your bills are tax invoices with GST on them.
- **Composition scheme**: your bills are a Bill of Supply, with no GST charged to the customer.
- **Not registered**: a plain receipt, no GST.

For a registered shop, type [[2]] the **GSTIN** and the [[3]] **State** fills in by itself from its first two digits. The state decides whether a bill carries CGST+SGST or IGST.

![The GST part of the form. The three choices are marked 1, GSTIN 2 and State 3.](3b-gst.webp "A registered shop with no GSTIN saved cannot print tax invoices until it is filled in.")

:::note GST is optional
You do not have to fill in anything about GST. A shop with no GSTIN saved prints plain receipts with no GST, whatever it chose above and whatever rate its products carry. Choose a registration and save your GSTIN only if your shop is GST registered. The ScaleEzy POS till follows exactly what you save here. See [Connect your POS till](/help/settings/pos-billing-counter).
:::

## 5. Check the preview and save

1. [[1]] The **Preview** shows the top of a purchase order with what you typed. It changes as you type.
2. Press [[2]] **Save changes**.

![The letterhead preview with the logo, shop name, address, phone, email and GSTIN, marked 1. The Save changes button is marked 2.](4-preview-and-save.webp "When everything is saved, the button says Saved.")

Changed your mind before saving? Press **Discard** to go back to the saved details.

## Where your details are used

| Where | What is printed |
|---|---|
| Counter receipt | Logo, shop name, address, phone, GSTIN and the receipt footer |
| Purchase order PDF | Logo, shop name, address, phone, email and GSTIN |
| Goods receipt PDF | Logo, shop name, address, phone, email and GSTIN |
| Bills from the ScaleEzy POS till | Logo, shop name, address, phone, GSTIN and the receipt footer. A connected till always takes them from here, so change them here, not on the till |
| Settings, for everyone | The logo in place of the letter in the round picture |

:::note A store's own address comes first
If a store has its own address or phone in **Stock locations**, the receipt from that store prints the store's address and phone. The shop address is used when the store has none. See [Stores and godowns](/help/settings/locations).
:::

## What your team sees

Your staff see the shop's logo, name and shop ID in **Your profile**, at the top of their **Settings**. They do not get the **Name, logo and bill details** link, so they cannot change them.

![A team member's profile: the shop logo, their name and role, the shop name Lakshmi Silks and the shop ID. On the right, their login email, a password set by whoever manages the team, and Sign out of other devices.](5-what-others-see.webp "Staff also cannot change their own password. See Your password.")

## Common problems

:::faq I don't see Shop details
Only the shop owner sees this form. If you are the owner, check that you signed in with the owner's email.
:::

:::faq "That image is larger than 2MB"
Your logo file is too big. Save it smaller (for example 500 × 500 pixels), then choose it again.
:::

:::faq "A GSTIN is 15 characters, like 27ABCDE1234F1Z5"
The GST number has a typing mistake. Check it on your GST certificate. It has exactly 15 letters and numbers.
:::

:::faq The receipt footer is not on my purchase order
That is correct. The receipt footer prints only on counter receipts.
:::
