Link your shop's **own WhatsApp number** once. After that, pressing **Send on WhatsApp** sends the PDF straight to the supplier or customer, from your number. They see it in the same chat as always.

## 1. Link your shop's WhatsApp

1. In the menu on the left, click **Settings**, then **WhatsApp**.
2. Click **Link by scanning a QR code**.
3. On the shop's phone, open **WhatsApp** and tap **⋮** (or **Settings**) > **Linked devices** > **Link a device**.
4. Point the phone at the code on the screen.

The screen says **Linked** with the last four digits of the number. The phone keeps working as normal.

:::tip No camera, or the code will not scan?
Click **Link with a code instead**, type the shop phone's WhatsApp number and click **Get the code**. On the phone, tap **Link with phone number instead** and type the 8 letters shown.
:::

## 2. Send a document

**Send on WhatsApp** is on:

- a **purchase order**, and each **delivery received** on it (goes to the supplier),
- a **counter sale**, on the order page and the receipt (goes to the customer),
- a **finished return** (goes to the customer).

Press it once. It goes to the number saved for that supplier or customer, never to a number typed at the time. Under the button you see where it has got to:

- **Waiting to send**: in the queue. Messages go a few seconds apart.
- **Sent**: WhatsApp has it. A purchase order is then marked **Sent** by itself.
- **Delivered**: it reached their phone.
- **Read**: they opened it.
- **Not sent**: it did not go, and the reason is written next to it.

If your shop's WhatsApp is not linked, the button says **Share on WhatsApp** instead. It opens WhatsApp on this computer or phone with the message typed, and you attach the PDF yourself.

## 3. The Day Book every night

The owner can have the Day Book sent to them every night, as a PDF on WhatsApp, from **ScaleEzy's** number.

1. In **Settings > WhatsApp**, under **The Day Book, every night**, tick **Send me the Day Book every night**.
2. The time is **22:00** (10 pm). Change it if your shop closes later.
3. Type your WhatsApp number and click **Save**.

To check it now, click **Send me today's now**. Only the owner sees this.

## 4. Unlink

Click **Unlink this number**, then **Unlink**. Documents stop going from it until a number is linked again. You can also remove **ScaleEzy** from **Linked devices** on the phone.

## Who can do this

The owner, and admins who manage the team, link and unlink the number. Anyone who can send a purchase order, receive goods, see orders or see returns can press **Send on WhatsApp** for that document. Only the owner sets the nightly Day Book.

## Common problems

:::faq The phone says "Couldn't link device"
Wait a few seconds and scan the **new** code that appears on the screen. The first scan sometimes only prepares the link.
:::

:::faq Does it stay linked, or do I link it every day?
It stays linked, for days and weeks, whoever signs in to ScaleEzy. It only stops if someone removes it from **Linked devices**, WhatsApp is removed or moved to another phone, or the phone has no internet for about 14 days. If it stops, the owner is told by email, and on WhatsApp at their Day Book number. Link it again the same way.
:::

:::faq Does the phone have to be on?
No, messages go even when the phone is off. But open WhatsApp on that phone at least once every few days, or WhatsApp unlinks it.
:::

:::faq It says "Waiting to send" for a long time
The reason is shown under it. **Not connected**: link the shop's WhatsApp again, and it goes then. **Today's limit is used up**: to keep your number safe from being blocked, a newly linked number sends up to 40 messages a day for its first two weeks, then up to 200. The rest go after midnight. Anything not sent within a day is dropped, never sent late.
:::

:::faq "Not sent: This number is not on WhatsApp"
The number saved for that supplier or customer has no WhatsApp. Check the number on their page, or use **Download PDF** and send it another way.
:::

:::faq "This person replied STOP"
They asked not to get WhatsApp messages from your shop, so none are sent to them. Contact them another way.
:::

:::faq It went to the wrong person
It goes to the number saved on the supplier's or customer's page. Correct the number there, then send it again. Pointing at the button shows who it goes to.
:::

:::faq I don't see WhatsApp in Settings
Only the owner and admins who manage the team see it. Ask them to link the shop's number.
:::

:::faq The button says Share on WhatsApp, not Send
The shop's WhatsApp is not linked yet, or WhatsApp is not switched on for ScaleEzy. Share still works: it opens WhatsApp with the message typed.
:::

:::faq Can ScaleEzy read our chats?
No. Only the documents you send from ScaleEzy go through it, and ScaleEzy keeps no copy of your chats. It keeps what was sent, when, and whether it arrived, with only the last four digits of the number.
:::

:::faq We have two stores. Two numbers?
One number for the whole shop. Every store sends from it.
:::
