All of this is in **Settings → General Info**. Your name, email and **Access level** are at the top. Password and other devices are further down.

![General Info. Change password is marked 1 and Sign out other devices is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Scroll down past Shop details to find them.")

- [[1]] **Change password** is only for the owner.
- [[2]] **Sign out other devices** is for everyone.

## Change your name

Everyone can change their own name.

1. At the top of **General Info**, click **Edit profile**.
2. [[1]] Type your name in **Full name**.
3. Press [[2]] **Save changes**. You see **Profile updated**.

![The Edit profile form. Full name is marked 1 and Save changes is marked 2.](5-change-your-name.webp "Cancel closes the form without saving.")

**Access level** under your email shows your role, for example **Super Admin** or **Sales**. You cannot change your role or your email here. The owner or an admin changes roles in **Team & Users**.

## Change your password (owner)

1. In **Password**, click **Change password**.
2. [[1]] Type your **Current password**.
3. [[2]] Type the **New password**. It needs at least 8 characters. The eye button shows what you typed.
4. [[3]] Type the new password again in **Repeat new password**.
5. Press [[4]] **Change password**. It works only when all three boxes are filled correctly.

![The open Password form. Current password is marked 1, New password 2, Repeat new password 3 and Change password 4.](2-change-password.webp "Cancel closes the form without changing anything.")

You stay signed in on this screen. **Every other phone, till or computer** signed in with your account is signed out. Sign in there again with the new password.

:::note Why staff cannot change their own password
Staff passwords are set by the owner or an admin in **Team & Users**. If a staff member forgets their password, the owner or an admin can see it or set a new one. See [Change a role, reset a password, switch someone off](/help/settings/manage-team).
:::

## Sign out other devices (everyone)

Use this if you lost your phone, left a till signed in, or someone else may know your password.

1. In **Other devices**, click **Sign out other devices**.
2. Read the message, then press [[1]] **Sign them out**.

![The Other devices card asking to confirm. Sign them out is marked 1, with Cancel beside it.](3-sign-out-other-devices.webp "You stay signed in on this screen.")

Every other phone, till and computer signed in with **your account** is signed out at once.

:::warning Shared till login?
If several tills use the same login, this signs out all of those tills too. Everyone there must sign in again.
:::

The normal **Sign out** button at the bottom of the menu signs out only the screen you are using.

## What team members see

Staff do not see **Password**. They see a note that their password is set by whoever manages the team, and the **Other devices** card.

![General Info for a team member. A card says the password and role are set by whoever manages your team. Below it is the Other devices card.](4-team-member-view.webp "Staff can still sign out their other devices.")

## Common problems

:::faq "That is not your current password"
The first box is wrong. Type the password you use to sign in today. You stay signed in, so you can try again.
:::

:::faq "The two new passwords do not match"
The second and third boxes are different. Click the eye button to see them, and type them again.
:::

:::faq I forgot my password and cannot sign in
ScaleEzy does not send password reset emails. Staff: ask your owner or admin to set a new one. Owner: if another person in your shop is **SUPER ADMIN**, they can set a new one for you in **Team & Users**. If not, contact ScaleEzy. See [Sign in](/help/start/sign-in).
:::

:::faq "Enter your name."
The **Full name** box is empty. Type your name, then press **Save changes**.
:::

:::faq Another till suddenly asks everyone to sign in again
Someone pressed **Sign them out**, or the password of that login was changed. Sign in again on that till.
:::
