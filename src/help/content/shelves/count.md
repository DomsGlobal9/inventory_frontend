Count one shelf at a time, whenever a shelf looks wrong or a [shelf issue](/help/shelves/shelf-issues) asks for it.

:::note A shelf count never changes your stock
It only moves pieces between the shelf and **Not shelved**. To correct how many pieces your store has, use a [stock count](/help/inventory/stock-counts) or [correct stock](/help/inventory/correct-stock).
:::

Sales staff cannot count shelves.

## 1. Choose the shelf

1. In the menu on the left, click **Shelves**, then the [[1]] **Count** tab.
2. [[2]] Scan the label on the shelf you are counting.
3. No scanner? Under **Or choose it**, click [[3]] the shelf.

![The Count a shelf screen. The Count tab is marked 1, the scan box for the shelf label 2, and shelf FLOOR-C2-1 in the list 3.](1-open.webp "The list shows how many pieces are recorded on each shelf.")

## 2. Count every piece on it

1. [[1]] Scan the tag of every piece on the shelf, one by one. **Each scan counts one.**
2. Or type the number, or use [[2]] **−** and **+**, next to each item.
3. Leave [[3]] **I counted the whole shelf (anything not counted is not there)** ticked if you looked at the whole shelf. Untick it if you only counted some items.
4. Press [[4]] **Save count**.

![The count sheet for FLOOR-C2-1. The scan box is marked 1, the counter for Cotton Kurti size M showing 3 is marked 2, the whole shelf tick box 3 and Save count 4.](2-count-each-piece.webp "The sheet starts empty on purpose, so you count what is there, not what the screen expects.")

![The count sheet on a phone, with Cotton Kurti size M counted 3 and size S counted 5.](2-count-each-piece-phone.webp "Use a phone and scanner right at the shelf.")

If you scan something that is not recorded on this shelf, it is added to the sheet with a message.

## 3. Read the result

The top line says [[1]] how many items matched, and how many became shelf issues. Each item shows **Counted**, **was recorded** and **on the shelf now**.

![The result for FLOOR-C2-1: 1 of 2 matched, 1 raised as shelf issues, marked 1. Cotton Kurti size S, counted 5, was recorded 6, on the shelf now 5, is marked 2 with a message that 1 piece is now not shelved.](3-the-result.webp "Press Count another shelf to go on.")

What a difference [[2]] means:

| You counted | What ScaleEzy does |
|---|---|
| The same as recorded | Nothing. A green tick. |
| **Fewer** than recorded | The shelf is set to your count. The missing pieces become **Not shelved**, because they are somewhere in the store. A shelf issue **Not found on the shelf** asks someone to find them. |
| **More** than recorded | The extra pieces are taken from **Not shelved**. If the store's stock is too low to allow that, a shelf issue asks you to correct the stock first, then count the shelf again. |

## Common problems

:::faq "… has shelves inside it. Count one of those."
You scanned a rack or an area. Scan the label of one shelf.
:::

:::faq "… does not match one item."
The tag could not be read, or it matches more than one item. Scan it again, or type the number next to the item instead.
:::

:::faq Items I did not count were set to 0
**I counted the whole shelf** was ticked, so anything not counted is taken as not there. Count the shelf again, or untick the box when you only count some items.
:::

:::faq Save count is greyed out
With **I counted the whole shelf** unticked, count at least one item first.
:::
