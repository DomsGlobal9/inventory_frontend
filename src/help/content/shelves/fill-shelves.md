When you first set up racks, ScaleEzy knows your shelves but not what is on them. Every piece shows as **Not shelved**. **Fill shelves** is the one walk round the shop that fixes that.

This never changes how much stock you have. It only says **where** each piece is.

Do it on a phone. Two or three people can work at the same time on different racks.

## 1. Open Fill shelves

1. In the menu on the left, tap **Shelves**.
2. Tap the [[1]] **Fill shelves** tab.

At the top you see [[2]] how many shelves are done, and how many pieces are still on no shelf. Under it is [[3]] the shelf to go to next.

![Fill your shelves before anything is recorded. The Fill shelves tab is marked 1, the line saying how many shelves are done 2, and the button for the next shelf 3.](1-open.webp "Every piece starts as not on a shelf. Your stock numbers do not change.")

## 2. Stand at a shelf

ScaleEzy shows the next shelf in walking order, so you move round the shop in a sensible line instead of running back and forth.

- Tap **I'm at this shelf**, or
- Scan the label on the shelf, if you have printed them.

## 3. Add what is really on that shelf

For each item on the shelf:

1. [[1]] **Scan its price tag**, or type part of the name, the SKU or the number under the barcode.
2. If one item matches, it is added for you. If the same code matches a few sizes or colours, ScaleEzy asks which one: choose the right one, so the wrong size does not end up on the shelf.
3. [[2]] Type **how many** of that item are on this shelf.

Add as many items as the shelf holds. The same item added twice becomes one line. Then tap [[3]] **Done with this shelf**.

![Shelf FLOOR-R1-1 with one saree added. The search box is marked 1, how many 2 and Done with this shelf 3.](2-add-what-is-there.webp "Scan every item on the shelf, then tap Done once.")

## 4. Done with this shelf

Tap **Done with this shelf**. Everything on that shelf is saved together, and ScaleEzy moves you to the next shelf.

If one line asks for more pieces than ScaleEzy has, **nothing on that shelf is saved yet**. [[1]] The line that needs a change says so, with [[2]] a shortcut such as **Use 6**. Everything else you typed stays on the screen. Fix that one line and tap **Done** again.

![A line asking for 99 when only 6 are not on a shelf yet. The message is marked 1 and the Use 6 shortcut 2.](3-a-line-needs-a-change.webp "Nothing on this shelf is saved until every line is right.")

## 5. Nothing on the shelf, or not now

- **Skip this shelf** puts it on a short list to come back to. It is not counted as done.
- **Leave this shelf** simply closes it. Nothing is lost; you can open it again later.

An empty shelf that you have checked is still worth finishing: add nothing and tap **Skip this shelf**, so ScaleEzy knows somebody has been there.

## 6. While you are filling

Your shop keeps working. Until the walk is finished:

- A till sale takes a piece that is **on no shelf** first. A shelf you counted a minute ago is not quietly reduced.
- New stock arriving simply adds to the pieces waiting to be put on a shelf.
- If nothing is left off the shelves, a sale does come off a shelf, and ScaleEzy tells you which shelf to check in **Shelf issues**.

## 7. Finishing

When every shelf is done, the first fill finishes by itself.

If you stop half way, the owner or a manager can tap **Finished** at any time. If shelves were skipped, ScaleEzy names them first and asks once more, because "skipped" is not the same as "done". Tap [[1]] **Finish** to end it anyway.

![The question after Finished, with one shelf skipped and 6 pieces still not on a shelf. Finish is marked 1.](4-finished.webp "Cancel keeps the first fill going.")

After it is finished, a till sale goes back to taking from shop-floor shelves first, and you use **Put away** for new stock from then on.

Reorganised the whole shop later? The owner can tap **Fill again** and walk round once more.

## Common problems

:::faq It says ScaleEzy has none of these left to put on a shelf
Every piece ScaleEzy has of that item is already on some shelf. Either it is on another shelf (check **Where is it?**), or the shop really has more than ScaleEzy knows about. Put those pieces aside and do a **stock count** for that item; never guess a number here.
:::

:::faq My shelf holds more than the "Holds about" number
That is only a note to help you plan. ScaleEzy saves what is really there and shows a warning. Nothing is refused.
:::

:::faq Two of us opened the same shelf
Whoever opens it sees a line saying who was there and when. Both of you can carry on: what each person adds is added, never replaced.
:::

:::faq The phone lost internet in the middle
Nothing is saved until you tap **Done with this shelf**. Tap it again when the phone is back online. Tapping twice never puts the pieces away twice.
:::

:::faq Some items are still on no shelf at the end
That is normal. They may be in a pile, at the tailor, or new stock waiting. Put them away as usual with **Put away**.
:::

:::faq Some items need a stock check
Their numbers do not add up: ScaleEzy says the shop has less than nothing, or more is on the shelves than the shop holds. Those are shown separately and never hidden. Do a stock count for them.
:::
