The map only looks. It never changes your stock or your shelves.

## 1. Open the map

1. In the menu on the left, click **Shelves**, then the [[1]] **Map** tab.
2. [[2]] The search box is at the top.
3. Each area of the store is a box, like [[3]] **FLOOR** on the shop floor. Racks and cupboards sit side by side in walking order, with their shelves as small squares.

![The Rack map for Main Store. The Map tab is marked 1, the search box 2, and the FLOOR area with racks C1 and C2 is marked 3. The STORE back room area is below.](1-open.webp "Each area shows how many pieces it holds in all, at the top right.")

How to read a shelf square:

- The top line is the shelf number. The number under it is how many pieces are on it.
- A dot means the shelf is empty.
- A darker square holds more pieces than a lighter one.
- Faded squares are switched off.

## 2. Search to light up shelves

Type part of a name, a SKU or a colour, or scan a tag.

- [[1]] The line under the box says how many shelves hold it.
- [[2]] Those shelves turn green and show how many pieces of **that item** are there.

![A search for Kanchipuram. The words Found on 3 shelves are marked 1, and the green shelf C1 1 with 8 pieces is marked 2.](2-search-lights-up.webp "If it says Not on any shelf here, the pieces are Not shelved, or in another store.")

## 3. See what is on a shelf

Click any shelf square. A box shows everything recorded on that shelf.

- Click an item to light up every shelf that holds it.
- **Move from here** opens [Move](/help/shelves/move) with this shelf chosen. Only people who can move stock see it.

![The box for shelf FLOOR-C1-1, Silk sarees, 8 pieces: Kanchipuram Silk Saree Maroon 3 and Red 5, with a Move from here button.](3-what-is-on-a-shelf.webp "Close it with the X, or click outside the box.")

## Common problems

:::faq No racks or shelves here yet
This store has no racks set up. See [Set up racks and print labels](/help/shelves/set-up-racks), or check the store at the top of the screen.
:::

:::faq Nothing lights up
The item is not on any shelf in this store. Try [Where is it?](/help/shelves/where-is-it) with **Look in every location** ticked.
:::

:::faq The racks are in the wrong order
The map follows the walking order. The owner or a manager can change it in **Racks & shelves** with the up and down arrows.
:::
