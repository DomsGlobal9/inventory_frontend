Moving pieces between shelves never changes your stock. It only changes *where* the pieces are.

Sales staff cannot move stock. They see the words **You cannot move stock between shelves**.

## 1. Open Move

1. In the menu on the left, click **Shelves**, then the [[1]] **Move** tab.
2. The cursor is in [[2]] the scan box. **Scan the label on the shelf the pieces are on now.**

No scanner? Click **Or choose the shelf** and click the shelf in the list. You can type part of the address, like *C1*, to find it.

![The Move stock screen. The Move tab is marked 1 and the scan box for the shelf the pieces are on is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Step 1 is the shelf the pieces are leaving. Step 3, on the right, stays empty until you choose an item.")

:::tip Coming from Where is it?
The **Move** button next to a shelf in [Where is it?](/help/shelves/where-is-it) opens this screen with the shelf and the item already chosen. Go straight to step 3.
:::

## 2. Choose the item

You now see the shelf and everything recorded on it, with how many pieces.

- Wrong shelf? Click [[1]] **Another shelf**.
- Click [[2]] the item you are moving. If the shelf holds only one item, it is chosen for you.
- To empty the whole shelf at once, use [[3]] **Move everything on this shelf**. See step 5.

![The From side for shelf FLOOR-C1-1. Another shelf is marked 1, Kanchipuram Silk Saree Red with 7 pieces is marked 2, and Move everything on this shelf is marked 3.](2-from-shelf-and-item.webp "The big number is how many pieces are recorded on this shelf.")

## 3. Say how many, then scan where they go

1. [[1]] Use **−** and **+** to set how many pieces you are moving. **All** takes every piece of this item on the shelf.
2. [[2]] **Scan the label on the shelf they are going to.** The pieces move straight away.
3. No scanner? Open [[3]] **Or choose the shelf** and click the shelf. That also moves them straight away.
4. Taking the pieces off shelves completely, for example to the packing table? Click [[4]] **Take … off the shelf (back to Not shelved)**.

![The To side. The quantity 2 is marked 1, the scan box 2, Or choose the shelf 3 and Take 2 off the shelf (back to Not shelved) 4.](3-how-many-and-where.webp "Check the number before you scan. The scan saves at once.")

![On a phone the To side is under the From side. The quantity is marked 1 and the scan box is marked 2.](3-how-many-and-where-phone.webp "On a phone, scroll down to the To side after choosing the item.")

## 4. Check it worked

A green message at the bottom of the screen says [[1]] how many moved and where, for example *2 moved to FLOOR-C1-2*. The screen is ready for the next item.

![The message 2 moved to FLOOR-C1-2, marked 1.](4-moved.webp "Nothing else to press. The move is saved.")

## 5. Move everything on a shelf

Emptying a shelf, or swapping two racks around?

1. On the From side, click **Move everything on this shelf**. You only see it when the shelf holds more than one item.
2. On the right, scan the label of the new shelf, or click it in the list.
3. ScaleEzy asks **Move everything?** and says how many pieces go where. Press [[1]] **Move everything**.

![The Move everything question: every item on FLOOR-C1-1, 9 pieces, moves to STORE-R1-2. The Move everything button is marked 1.](5-move-everything.webp "Press Cancel if you chose the wrong shelf. Nothing has moved yet.")

## Common problems

:::faq The pieces are already on that shelf.
You scanned the same shelf the pieces are on. Scan the shelf they are going to.
:::

:::faq "… is in another location. Use a transfer for that."
Moves only work inside one store. To send pieces to the godown or another branch, make a [transfer](/help/transfers/transfers).
:::

:::faq "… has shelves inside it. Scan one of those."
You scanned a rack or an area, not a shelf. Scan the label on the shelf itself.
:::

:::faq The item is not on the shelf I scanned
ScaleEzy only lists what is recorded there. Search the item in [Where is it?](/help/shelves/where-is-it) to see which shelf it is really on. If the shelf is wrong, [count the shelf](/help/shelves/count).
:::

:::faq I moved the wrong number
Just move them back the same way. Nothing is lost and your stock does not change.
:::
