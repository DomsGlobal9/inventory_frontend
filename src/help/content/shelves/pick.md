**Pick** makes one walking list for many orders at once. ScaleEzy tells you which shelf to go to next, in the order you walk the shop, and what to take there for which order.

- The owner, an admin and stock room staff can pick and send orders out.
- An inventory manager can pick, but not send out. They see *Someone who can send orders out finishes this.*
- Sales staff cannot pick.

## 1. Choose the orders

1. In the menu on the left, click **Shelves**, then the [[1]] **Pick** tab.
2. You see every order in this store that is confirmed and still has pieces to send. Click [[2]] each order you want to pick. A green tick shows it is chosen. **Select** chooses them all (up to 30 at a time).
3. Press [[3]] **Pick … orders**.

![The Pick screen with three orders waiting to go out from Main Store, all ticked. The Pick tab is marked 1, order SO-000003 is marked 2 and Pick 3 orders is marked 3.](1-choose-orders.webp "The big number on the right is how many pieces the order needs.")

## 2. Walk the shelves

The list is in walking order. The shop floor comes first, then the back room.

- [[1]] The shelf to go to, in large letters. Its number on the left is the order you visit it in.
- [[2]] The scan box tells you where to go next. **Scan each piece's tag** as you take it. Each scan counts one piece. Scanning the shelf label checks you are at the right shelf.
- No scanner? Press [[3]] **Picked** when you have all the pieces on that line.
- The piece is not on the shelf? Press [[4]] **Not here**. See step 3.

![The walking list: 0 of 6 picks, 7 pieces for 3 orders. The shelf FLOOR-C1-1 is marked 1, the scan box saying Go to FLOOR-C1-1, scan the item is marked 2, Picked is marked 3 and Not here is marked 4.](2-walk-the-shelves.webp "Each line shows the item, its SKU and which order it is for.")

![The walking list on a phone, with the first shelf FLOOR-C1-1 and its Picked and Not here buttons.](2-walk-the-shelves-phone.webp "Carry the phone and a scanner as you walk. The bar at the top shows how many picks are done.")

Pieces that are in the store but on no shelf are listed at the end, under **Not on a shelf — find these in the location**. If the store does not have enough of an item at all, a red box **Not enough here** lists it at the top.

## 3. When a piece is not on the shelf

Press **Not here** on that line. ScaleEzy:

- marks the line [[1]] *not found — reported*,
- adds a [shelf issue](/help/shelves/shelf-issues) so someone counts that shelf,
- does **not** send that piece out, and does not change your stock.

![The line for Anarkali Suit Set on back room shelf STORE-R1-2, with the words 1 not found — reported marked 1.](3-not-here.webp "The order keeps waiting for that piece. Pick it again once it is found.")

## 4. Send out what you picked

At the bottom, press [[1]] **Send out picked**. You do not have to finish the whole list first. Only the pieces you picked are sent.

![The bottom bar says Everything is picked. The Send out picked button is marked 1.](4-send-out-picked.webp "The shelves each piece came from are recorded on the order.")

## 5. Check it worked

A green message says how many orders were sent out, and you are back at the order list. An order that is still missing pieces stays in the list, marked [[1]] **partly sent**.

![The order list after sending. Only SO-000005 is still waiting, with the words partly sent marked 1.](5-sent.webp "Sent orders also show as sent in Orders.")

## Common problems

:::faq Nothing waiting to go out
Only confirmed orders from the store chosen at the top of the screen are listed. Check the store name, and check the order is confirmed in **Orders**.
:::

:::faq "… is not on this pick list, or all of it is picked."
The tag you scanned is not needed for these orders, or you already have enough of it. Put the piece back.
:::

:::faq I cannot press Send out picked
Nothing is picked yet, or your role cannot send orders out. If you see *Someone who can send orders out finishes this*, ask the owner or the stock room.
:::

:::faq I closed the page halfway
Ticks on the walking list are not saved until you press **Send out picked**. Choose the orders again and pick again. Nothing was sent twice.
:::
