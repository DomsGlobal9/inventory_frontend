## 1. Open Put away

1. In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Shelves**.
2. At the top, click the [[2]] **Put away** tab.

![The Shelves screen. Shelves is marked 1 in the left menu and the Put away tab is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Shelves in the menu, then the Put away tab.")

## 2. Choose the item

You see everything waiting for a shelf, with how many pieces. Either:

- [[1]] **scan the price tag** with your scanner, or
- [[2]] **click the item** in the list.

![The Put away list. The scan box is marked 1 and Kanchipuram Silk Saree is marked 2.](2-choose-item.webp "The big number is how many pieces still need a shelf. The small number under it is how many this store has in all.")

## 3. Say how many, and which shelf

1. [[1]] Use **−** and **+** to set how many pieces go on this shelf.
2. [[2]] **Scan the label on the shelf.** It saves straight away. You are done.
3. No scanner? Under **Already kept on**, click [[3]] the shelf that already holds this item. Or open **Or choose another shelf**.

![The item panel. The quantity is marked 1, the shelf label box 2 and the shelf FLOOR-C1-1 is marked 3.](3-how-many-which-shelf.webp "SHOP FLOOR shelves are where customers shop. BACK ROOM is your store room.")

:::tip Same item on two shelves?
Put some on the first shelf, then do step 3 again for the rest. The list always shows what is still left.
:::

## 4. Press Put away

If you clicked a shelf instead of scanning, check the line at the bottom, for example *Put 2 on FLOOR-C1-1*, and press [[1]] **Put away**.

![The Put away button at the bottom right is marked 1.](4-press-put-away.webp "The item leaves the list once all its pieces are on shelves.")

## 5. Check it worked

Open the **Where is it?** tab and scan the same tag. [[1]] The shelf now shows the new number.

![Where is it? for Kanchipuram Silk Saree. The FLOOR-C1-1 shelf is marked 1.](5-check-where-is-it.webp "Shop floor shelves are always listed first.")

:::note Put away never changes your stock
It only records *where* the pieces are. Pieces on shelves + Not shelved = your stock.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq The item is not in the list
Either all of it is already on shelves, or it was received at a different store. Check the store name at the top of the screen.
:::

:::faq "This label belongs to another store"
You are working in the wrong store. Change the store at the top of the screen, then scan again.
:::

:::faq I put it on the wrong shelf
Use [Move between shelves](/help/shelves/move). Nothing is lost and your stock does not change.
:::

:::faq I don't see Put away
Your role cannot put stock away. Ask your shop owner to give you the permission in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
:::
