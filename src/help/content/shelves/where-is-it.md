Everyone who can see shelves can use **Where is it?**, including sales staff. It works on a computer, a tablet and a phone.

## 1. Open Where is it?

In the menu on the left, click [[1]] **Shelves**. **Where is it?** opens first. The cursor is already in [[2]] the scan box.

![The Where is it screen. Shelves in the menu is marked 1 and the scan box is marked 2.](1-open.webp "Point the scanner at a tag straight away. No need to click the box first.")

## 2. Scan the price tag

Scan the tag on the piece, or type part of its name, SKU or colour. The answer appears as you type.

- [[1]] Each shelf holding it shows its address in large letters, and how many pieces are there. **Shop floor** shelves come first, in the order you walk the shop.
- [[2]] **Back room** shelves come after.
- At the top right you see how many pieces this store has in all, and how many are held for orders.

![Where is it for Kanchipuram Silk Saree, Red. The shop floor shelf FLOOR-C1-1 with 5 pieces is marked 1 and the back room shelf STORE-R1-1 with 6 pieces is marked 2.](2-scan-the-tag.webp "The first shop floor shelf is shaded green: go there first.")

Stock room staff also see a **Move** button on each shelf. See [Move between shelves](/help/shelves/move).

## 3. Pieces that are Not shelved

[[1]] **Not shelved** means the pieces are in this store but nobody has put them on a shelf yet, for example a delivery that just arrived. Look near where deliveries are unpacked.

![Chanderi Dupatta with all 11 pieces Not shelved, marked 1, and a Put away button.](3-not-shelved.webp "Put away sends you straight to putting this item on a shelf.")

If the line says *this location has no shelves set up*, the store has no racks yet. See [Set up racks and print labels](/help/shelves/set-up-racks).

## 4. Scan a shelf label to see what is on it

Scan the QR label on a shelf, or type its address, like **FLOOR-C1-1**, and press **Enter**. You see everything recorded on that shelf.

Click [[1]] an item to see every shelf that holds it. **Move from here** opens Move with this shelf already chosen.

![What is on shelf FLOOR-C1-1: Kanchipuram Silk Saree Red, 5 pieces, marked 1, and Kanchipuram Silk Saree Maroon, 4 pieces. Move from here is at the top right.](4-scan-a-shelf.webp "Close it with the X to search again.")

## 5. Look in other stores too

Tick [[1]] **Look in every location, not just Main Store**. Each store that has the piece is listed with its own shelves.

![The Look in every location box is ticked, marked 1, above the result for Main Store.](5-every-location.webp "Useful when a customer asks whether another branch or the godown has it.")

:::note Where is it? never changes anything
It only looks. Your stock and your shelves stay as they are.
:::

## Common problems

:::faq Nothing matches
Check the spelling, try the SKU or the colour, or scan the tag again. Also check the store at the top of the screen, or tick **Look in every location**.
:::

:::faq The shelf says 3 but I only find 2
Someone may have moved pieces without recording it. Look on nearby shelves. If they are really missing, [count the shelf](/help/shelves/count) so the difference is recorded.
:::

:::faq "No shelf matches that label or address."
The label may belong to a shelf that was removed, or it is damaged. Type the address printed on it instead, or ask your manager to reprint it from **Racks & shelves**.
:::

:::faq I don't see the Move or Put away buttons
Sales staff can find stock but not move it. Ask someone from the stock room, or your owner for the permission.
:::
