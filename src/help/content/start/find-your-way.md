Every screen in ScaleEzy has the same frame around it. Learn these seven places once and you can find anything.

![The ScaleEzy screen with seven places marked: 1 the menu, 2 the store you are in, 3 search, 4 alerts, 5 light or dark, 6 your name and sign out, 7 help for this screen.](1-the-screen.webp "The same frame is around every screen.")

## 1. The menu

[[1]] The menu on the left takes you to each part of ScaleEzy: Dashboard, Products, Orders, Returns, Inventory, Shelves and so on.

You only see the parts your job needs. If something is missing, your role doesn't include it. Ask your shop owner.

## 2. The store you are working in

[[2]] If your shop has more than one store or godown, choose which one you are working in here. Sales, stock numbers and shelves on every screen then belong to **that store**. See [Working with more than one store](/help/start/more-than-one-store).

:::warning Check this first
Most "the numbers look wrong" moments are simply the wrong store picked here.
:::

## 3. Search

[[3]] Type a product name or SKU, or **scan a barcode**, then press **Enter**. ScaleEzy opens that item straight away. If nothing matches, you see *Nothing found* and the code stays in the box so you can fix it. See [Use a barcode scanner](/help/start/barcode-scanner).

The search box is not shown on a phone or a narrow window.

## 4. Alerts

The bell (number 4 in the first picture) shows a red number when something is low or out of stock in the store you picked. Click it to see the **5 newest** alerts.

- Click an alert to mark it read and open the full alerts list.
- The pin keeps an alert marked, so it stands out. The **×** removes it.
- **Mark all read** clears the red number.

![The alerts list open under the bell, with Mark all read and View All Alerts.](2-alerts.webp "View All Alerts shows every item that is low or out of stock.")

When a new alert comes in, a small **New Inventory Alert!** box pops up under the bell with a short beep. Press **View Now** to open the list. The box goes away by itself after 8 seconds (4 seconds on a phone or small tablet), or as soon as you click, tap or scroll somewhere else. The red number stays on the bell.

People whose role cannot see stock, like sales staff, get no alerts. Their bell list stays empty.

Read more: [Low stock alerts](/help/inventory/low-stock-alerts).

## 5. Light or dark

[[5]] Switch between a light and a dark screen. Your choice is remembered on this device.

## 6. Your name and Sign out

[[6]] At the bottom of the menu you see who is signed in. The arrow button **signs you out**. On a shared counter computer, always sign out at the end of your shift.

Your own details and password are in **Settings → General Info**. See [Your profile, password and other devices](/help/settings/your-password).

## 7. Help for this screen

[[7]] The **?** button opens this guide at the page for the screen you are on. It opens in a **new tab**, so a half-finished sale or order stays as it is. Close the tab to go back.

## On a phone or tablet

The menu is hidden to give you more room. Tap [[1]] the **☰** button at the top left to open it.

![ScaleEzy on a phone. The menu button at the top left is marked 1.](3-on-a-phone-phone.webp "Tap outside the menu to close it.")

:::note New sale needs a bigger screen
Counter sales work on a computer or tablet, not on a phone. Everything else works on a phone.
:::
