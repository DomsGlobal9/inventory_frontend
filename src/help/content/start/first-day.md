Pick your job below. Do the guides in order, and you will know everything you need for a normal day.

## Owner

You set the shop up once, then keep an eye on it.

1. [Shop name, logo and letterhead](/help/settings/shop-details)
2. [Stores and godowns](/help/settings/locations)
3. [Add a product](/help/products/add-a-product), or [import many from a sheet](/help/products/import-products)
4. [Suppliers](/help/purchase-orders/suppliers) and [raise a purchase order](/help/purchase-orders/create-a-purchase-order)
5. [Add your team](/help/settings/add-team-member) and [choose what each role can do](/help/settings/roles)
6. [Set up racks and print labels](/help/shelves/set-up-racks) *(if you use shelves)*
7. [Your Dashboard](/help/dashboard/dashboard) and [the Day Book](/help/settings/day-book)

## Sales

You help customers and make sales.

1. [Find your way around](/help/start/find-your-way)
2. [Where is it?](/help/shelves/where-is-it): find any piece in seconds
3. [Make a counter sale](/help/orders/new-sale)
4. [Print or reprint a receipt](/help/orders/receipts)
5. [Customers](/help/customers/customers)

## Stock room

You receive goods, keep shelves in order and send orders out.

1. [Find your way around](/help/start/find-your-way)
2. [Receive a delivery](/help/purchase-orders/receive-a-delivery)
3. [Put away stock](/help/shelves/put-away)
4. [Pick online orders](/help/shelves/pick) and [send them out](/help/orders/send-online-orders)
5. [Book in a return](/help/returns/book-a-return) and [finish it](/help/returns/finish-a-return)
6. [Count a shelf](/help/shelves/count)

## Stock and buying

You look after products, stock levels and suppliers.

1. [Add a product](/help/products/add-a-product)
2. [See your stock](/help/inventory/see-your-stock)
3. [Low stock alerts](/help/inventory/low-stock-alerts) and [reorder suggestions](/help/purchase-orders/reorder)
4. [Raise and send a purchase order](/help/purchase-orders/create-a-purchase-order)
5. [Stock counts](/help/inventory/stock-counts) and [correct stock](/help/inventory/correct-stock)
6. [Move stock between stores](/help/transfers/transfers)
