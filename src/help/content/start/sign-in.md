## 1. Open ScaleEzy

Go to **inventory.scaleezy.com** in any browser: Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a computer, tablet or phone.

:::tip Save it
Add it to your bookmarks, or on a phone use **Add to Home screen**, so it opens like an app.
:::

## 2. Type your email and password

1. [[1]] Type the **email address** your shop owner gave you.
2. [[2]] Type your **password**. Tap the eye to see what you typed.
3. Press [[3]] **Sign in**.

![The Sign in screen. The email box is marked 1, the password box 2 and the Sign in button 3.](1-sign-in.webp "You stay signed in on this device until you sign out.")

## 3. If your email is used in more than one shop

Some people work for two shops with the same email. ScaleEzy then asks **which shop** to sign into. Click the shop's name. To switch later, sign out and sign in again.

## Forgot your password?

ScaleEzy does not send reset emails. Your **shop owner or manager** sets a new password for you in **Settings → Team & Users**. It takes them a few seconds.

1. Press [[1]] **Forgot your password?** under the Sign in button to see this on the screen too.
2. Ask your owner or manager for a new password.

![Forgot your password? opened under the Sign in button, marked 1.](2-forgot-password.webp)

:::note Owners
To reset someone's password, see [Change a role, reset a password, switch someone off](/help/settings/manage-team).
:::

## New shop? Request access

**Request access**, under the Sign in button, is for a shop that does not use ScaleEzy yet. You fill in **Shop name**, **Your name**, **Phone**, **Email** and, if you like, a message, then press **Send request**.

This only sends an enquiry to the ScaleEzy team. It does **not** make an account, and you cannot sign in with it. We get in touch and set your shop up with you. If your shop already uses ScaleEzy, ask your shop owner for a login instead.

## Common problems

:::faq "Invalid credentials"
The email or password is wrong, or your account has been switched off. Check for a space before or after your email, and that Caps Lock is off. Still stuck? Ask your owner. They can set a new password or switch your account back on.
:::

:::faq I was signed out by myself
This happens when your password is changed, when your account is switched off, or when someone chose **Sign out other devices**. Sign in again with your current password.
:::

:::faq "Too many requests from this address"
After a lot of tries in a short time, ScaleEzy pauses for a minute to protect your shop. Wait a minute and try again.
:::

:::faq My shop doesn't have ScaleEzy yet
Click **Request access** on the sign in screen and tell us about your shop. We will get in touch.
:::
