ScaleEzy keeps track of every piece in your shop: what you have, where it is, what it cost, and what you sold. Everyone in your team signs in with their own login and sees only what their job needs.

![Your Dashboard in ScaleEzy, showing stock value, low stock and recent activity.](1-dashboard.webp "The Dashboard is the first screen after you sign in.")

## How stock moves through your shop

![Diagram: a supplier sends goods, you receive them on a purchase order, put them on shelves, sell them at the counter or online, and returns come back to the shelves.](diagram-stock-flow.svg)

1. **Buy.** You raise a purchase order with your supplier. When the goods arrive, you *receive* them and your stock goes up.
2. **Keep.** If you use racks and shelves, you put each piece on a shelf, so anyone can find it in seconds.
3. **Sell.** At the counter with **New sale**, or online and on Shopify. Your stock goes down by itself.
4. **Returns.** When a customer brings something back, you check it and it goes back into stock.

### What happens to stock at each step

*Held* means the pieces are still in the store but kept aside for an order, so nobody else can sell them. On an order this shows as **RESERVED**.

| Step | What happens to your stock |
|---|---|
| **Counter sale** (New sale) | Goes down at once, in the store you sold from. |
| **Draft order** | Nothing changes. |
| **Confirmed order** | Nothing leaves the store yet, but the pieces are **held** for this order. |
| **Dispatched** (sent or handed over) | Goes down, and the hold is released. If only some pieces are sent, the rest stay held. |
| **Cancel Order** | Held pieces are free to sell again. Nothing was taken out, so stock does not change. |
| **Close rest of order** (part sent) | What was sent stays sold. The pieces not sent are free to sell again. |
| **Return completed** | Pieces marked **Restock** go back up, in the store the order came from. **Damaged** or **Scrap** pieces do not. |
| **Purchase order received** | Goes up in the store you receive into. You can receive part of an order now and the rest later. Sending a purchase order changes nothing. |
| **Transfer** | Goes down in one store and up in the other straight away. Only free pieces can be moved, not held ones. |

:::note Nothing is ever overwritten
Every change to your stock is saved as its own line, with who did it and when. You can always see why a number is what it is.
:::

## What you can do in ScaleEzy

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Everything you sell, with sizes, colours, prices, barcodes and photos |
| **Orders** | Counter sales, online orders and Shopify orders, and what was paid |
| **Returns** | Items customers bring back |
| **Inventory** | How much you have in each store, alerts for low stock, and stock counts |
| **Shelves** | Which rack and shelf every piece is on |
| **Transfers** | Moving stock between your stores |
| **Purchase Orders** | Buying from suppliers and receiving deliveries |
| **Offers** | Sales, discount codes and discounts at the counter |
| **Customers** | Your customers and what they bought |
| **Settings** | Your shop details, stores, team and roles |

:::tip You only see what your job needs
If a salesperson can't see **Purchase Orders**, that is on purpose. The shop owner decides who sees what in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
:::
